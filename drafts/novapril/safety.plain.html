Safety profile of NOVAPRIL

The safety of NOVAPRIL was evaluated in 982 patients with active SLE and lupus nephritis across the LUMINOS clinical programme, with a median treatment duration of 52 weeks.1 2

Well-characterised safety profile

The overall safety profile of NOVAPRIL was consistent with the known safety profile of B-cell modulating therapies. The rate of serious adverse events was numerically lower in the NOVAPRIL group compared with placebo (8.7% vs 9.2%).1 2

Key safety considerations

Infections: Patients should be monitored for signs and symptoms of infection during and after treatment with NOVAPRIL. NOVAPRIL should not be initiated in patients with active, severe infections.1

Immunoglobulin monitoring: Hypogammaglobulinaemia was reported in 2.8% of NOVAPRIL-treated patients. Measure immunoglobulin levels before initiation and periodically during treatment. Consider discontinuation if sustained, clinically significant hypogammaglobulinaemia develops.1

Vaccination: Avoid live vaccines during treatment and for 4 months after discontinuation. Non-live vaccines may be administered; however, the immune response may be attenuated.1

Special populations

Pregnancy and breastfeeding: NOVAPRIL is contraindicated during pregnancy. Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 4 months after the last dose. Breastfeeding is not recommended.1

Elderly patients: No dose adjustment is required in patients aged ≥65 years. Clinical experience in patients over 75 years is limited.1

Renal impairment: No dose adjustment is required for mild-to-moderate renal impairment. Use with caution in severe renal impairment due to limited data.1

Hepatic impairment: No dose adjustment is required for mild hepatic impairment. NOVAPRIL has not been studied in patients with moderate-to-severe hepatic impairment.1

Prescribing Information

NOVAPRIL (novaprylimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

NOVAPRIL 200 mg solution for injection in pre-filled pen. Each pre-filled pen contains 200 mg novaprylimab in 1 mL solution.

Indication: Treatment of adult patients with active systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) who have an inadequate response to standard therapy. Treatment of adult patients with active lupus nephritis (LN), Class III, IV, or V (± III or IV), as an add-on to standard therapy.

Posology: 200 mg subcutaneous injection at Weeks 0, 2, and 4 (loading), then 200 mg every 4 weeks (maintenance).

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Active, severe infection. Pregnancy.
