Dual BAFF/APRIL pathway inhibition

NOVAPRIL is the first therapy to simultaneously target both BAFF and APRIL, the two key cytokines driving pathogenic B-cell survival and autoantibody production in SLE.1

The role of B cells in SLE

Systemic lupus erythematosus is driven by a loss of B-cell tolerance, leading to the production of pathogenic autoantibodies that form immune complexes and drive tissue inflammation and organ damage.1 3 Two cytokines — BAFF and APRIL — play central roles in promoting the survival and differentiation of these autoreactive B cells.1

BAFF: B-cell activating factor

BAFF (also known as BLyS) is a key survival factor for transitional and mature B cells. Elevated BAFF levels are found in patients with active SLE and correlate with disease activity and anti-dsDNA antibody titres.1 3

BAFF signals through three receptors: BAFF-R (primary survival receptor on mature B cells), TACI, and BCMA. Blocking BAFF reduces survival signals to autoreactive B cells, but can lead to compensatory upregulation of APRIL signalling.1

APRIL: a proliferation-inducing ligand

APRIL promotes the survival of plasma cells and supports immunoglobulin class switching. In SLE, APRIL contributes to the persistence of long-lived plasma cells that produce pathogenic autoantibodies, particularly in affected tissues such as the kidneys.1

APRIL signals through TACI and BCMA. In patients treated with BAFF-only inhibitors, compensatory APRIL upregulation can sustain autoantibody-producing plasma cells, limiting the therapeutic response.1

The advantage of dual BAFF/APRIL inhibition

By simultaneously blocking both BAFF and APRIL, NOVAPRIL provides more comprehensive B-cell modulation than single-pathway inhibitors.1

Dual inhibition addresses the compensatory APRIL upregulation observed with BAFF-only blockade, reduces both transitional B-cell survival and plasma cell persistence, and leads to greater reductions in pathogenic autoantibodies and more robust clinical responses as demonstrated in the LUMINOS programme.1 4

Prescribing Information

NOVAPRIL (novaprylimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

NOVAPRIL 200 mg solution for injection in pre-filled pen. Each pre-filled pen contains 200 mg novaprylimab in 1 mL solution.

Indication: Treatment of adult patients with active systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) who have an inadequate response to standard therapy. Treatment of adult patients with active lupus nephritis (LN), Class III, IV, or V (± III or IV), as an add-on to standard therapy.

Posology: 200 mg subcutaneous injection at Weeks 0, 2, and 4 (loading), then 200 mg every 4 weeks (maintenance).

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Active, severe infection. Pregnancy.
