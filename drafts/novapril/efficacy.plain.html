Proven efficacy across the LUMINOS programme

NOVAPRIL demonstrated statistically significant improvements in SRI-4 response in SLE and complete renal response in lupus nephritis across two pivotal Phase III trials.1 2

The LUMINOS clinical programme

The LUMINOS programme evaluated the efficacy and safety of NOVAPRIL in over 1,400 patients with active SLE and lupus nephritis across two pivotal Phase III randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials.1 2

LUMINOS-1: SRI-4 response in active SLE

In LUMINOS-1, 61.2% of patients treated with NOVAPRIL achieved an SRI-4 response at Week 52, compared with 40.8% in the placebo group (OR 2.29, 95% CI: 1.74–3.02; P<0.001).1

NOVAPRIL also demonstrated a 52% reduction in severe flares (HR 0.48, 95% CI: 0.34–0.68; P<0.001) and enabled significantly more patients to achieve sustained corticosteroid reduction to ≤7.5 mg/day prednisone equivalent (48.3% vs 29.1%; P<0.001).1

LUMINOS-2: Complete renal response in lupus nephritis

In LUMINOS-2, 44.7% of patients with active lupus nephritis achieved a complete renal response at Week 52, compared with 22.1% in the placebo group (OR 2.87, 95% CI: 1.89–4.36; P<0.001).2

Patients treated with NOVAPRIL also demonstrated a 67% mean reduction in proteinuria at Week 52 (vs 31% with placebo) and faster time to complete renal response (median 24.1 weeks vs 38.7 weeks; P<0.001).2

Prescribing Information

NOVAPRIL (novaprylimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

NOVAPRIL 200 mg solution for injection in pre-filled pen. Each pre-filled pen contains 200 mg novaprylimab in 1 mL solution.

Indication: Treatment of adult patients with active systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) who have an inadequate response to standard therapy. Treatment of adult patients with active lupus nephritis (LN), Class III, IV, or V (± III or IV), as an add-on to standard therapy.

Posology: 200 mg subcutaneous injection at Weeks 0, 2, and 4 (loading), then 200 mg every 4 weeks (maintenance).

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Active, severe infection. Pregnancy.
