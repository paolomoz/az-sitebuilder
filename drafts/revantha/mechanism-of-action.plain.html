Dual PD-L1/TIGIT checkpoint blockade

REVANTHA is the first bispecific antibody to simultaneously block PD-L1 and TIGIT, two co-inhibitory pathways that tumours exploit to suppress anti-tumour immunity.1

How tumours evade the immune system

Cancer cells exploit immune checkpoint pathways to suppress T-cell and NK cell activity, creating an immunosuppressive tumour microenvironment. Two key inhibitory pathways — PD-L1 and TIGIT — act in concert to dampen anti-tumour immune responses, making simultaneous blockade a rational therapeutic strategy.1 3

PD-L1: suppressing T-cell attack

Tumour cells and antigen-presenting cells upregulate PD-L1, which binds PD-1 on T cells to deliver an inhibitory signal that suppresses T-cell proliferation, cytokine production, and cytotoxic activity.1 3

PD-L1 blockade has transformed NSCLC treatment, but many patients do not respond or develop resistance, suggesting additional inhibitory pathways limit the anti-tumour immune response.1

TIGIT: a complementary immune brake

TIGIT is an inhibitory receptor expressed on T cells and NK cells. It competes with the activating receptor CD226 (DNAM-1) for binding to CD155 and CD112 on tumour cells, tipping the balance towards immune suppression.1

TIGIT expression is upregulated in the tumour microenvironment and correlates with T-cell exhaustion. Notably, TIGIT is often co-expressed with PD-1 on tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes, providing the rationale for dual blockade.1

The bispecific advantage

By engineering both PD-L1 and TIGIT binding arms into a single molecule, REVANTHA ensures co-engagement of both checkpoint pathways on the same immune cell, providing synergistic T-cell reactivation.1

This bispecific design offers advantages over combination approaches: simultaneous target engagement, enhanced avidity through co-binding, a simplified single-agent regimen, and coordinated blockade that addresses PD-L1 monotherapy resistance mechanisms.1 4

Prescribing Information

REVANTHA (revantholimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

REVANTHA 20 mg/mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 1200 mg revantholimab in 60 mL.

Indication: First-line treatment of adult patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥1%) with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations, in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy. Monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC after prior platinum-based chemotherapy, whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%).

Posology: 1200 mg intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. First infusion over 60 minutes; subsequent infusions may be reduced to 30 minutes if tolerated.

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Pregnancy.
