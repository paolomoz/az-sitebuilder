The TRAJECTORY clinical programme

NOVATREL™ (telvisoran) demonstrated significant improvements in overall survival, progression-free survival, and response durability in first-line advanced NSCLC with PD-L1 TPS ≥50%.¹ ²

Designed to demonstrate a new trajectory in outcomes

The TRAJECTORY programme evaluated telvisoran as first-line monotherapy in patients with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%), with no EGFR or ALK aberrations. TRAJECTORY-1 established superiority over platinum-doublet chemotherapy across survival, response, and patient-reported outcomes. TRAJECTORY-LT confirmed the durability of benefit with 5-year follow-up.¹ ²

TRAJECTORY-1: Overall survival

TRAJECTORY-1: Median overall survival of 26.3 months

In TRAJECTORY-1, a randomised, open-label, Phase III trial in 637 adults with previously untreated locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC (PD-L1 TPS ≥50%, no EGFR/ALK), NOVATREL demonstrated superior overall survival compared with platinum-doublet chemotherapy.¹

NOVATREL delivered a 37% reduction in the risk of death (HR 0.63; P<0.001), with a median OS of 26.3 months versus 14.2 months. Response rates were nearly doubled (48% vs 27%), and median duration of response was 22.8 months compared with 6.3 months for chemotherapy.¹

TRAJECTORY-LT: 5-year survival

TRAJECTORY-LT: 5-year overall survival of 31.4%

Pre-specified 5-year extended follow-up of TRAJECTORY-1 confirmed the durable survival benefit of first-line NOVATREL in advanced NSCLC with high PD-L1 expression.²

At 5 years, 31.4% of patients treated with NOVATREL remained alive, compared with 14.6% with chemotherapy. Among patients who initially responded to NOVATREL, 18% maintained ongoing responses at the 5-year data cut-off, demonstrating the potential for long-lasting clinical benefit.²

Prescribing Information

NOVATREL (telvisoran) 200 mg/10 mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: First-line monotherapy for adults with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%), with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations. Dosage: 200 mg by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes every 3 weeks, or 400 mg every 6 weeks. Treat until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 24 months. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to telvisoran or any excipient. Warnings and precautions: Immune-mediated adverse reactions may be severe or fatal. Monitor for signs and symptoms of pneumonitis, hepatitis, colitis, endocrinopathies, nephritis, and skin reactions. Manage with dose interruption, corticosteroids, or permanent discontinuation as appropriate. Infusion-related reactions have been reported. Pregnancy and lactation: May cause foetal harm; effective contraception required during treatment and for 4 months after. Breastfeeding not recommended during treatment and for 4 months after. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, decreased appetite, rash, diarrhoea, nausea. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): cough, pruritus, dyspnoea, constipation, pneumonitis, hypothyroidism. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 1 vial (200 mg/10 mL): £2,630.00. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0512. Full prescribing information available from: AstraZeneca UK Ltd, 2 Pancras Square, London N1C 4AG.
