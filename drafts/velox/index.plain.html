VELOX® (veloxaban)

The first oral Factor XIa inhibitor for stroke prevention in atrial fibrillation. Non-inferior stroke protection with 41% fewer major bleeds versus rivaroxaban. Protection without compromise.¹

Explore the VELOCITY data

Non-inferior stroke prevention

In VELOCITY-AF, VELOX demonstrated non-inferior efficacy to rivaroxaban for the prevention of stroke and systemic embolism (HR 0.97; 95% CI: 0.83–1.14; P<0.001 for non-inferiority), in 14,228 patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation over a median 24-month treatment period.¹

41% fewer major bleeding events

VELOX significantly reduced ISTH major bleeding by 41% versus rivaroxaban (HR 0.59; 95% CI: 0.50–0.69; P<0.001). Intracranial haemorrhage was reduced by 60% (HR 0.40; P<0.001) and gastrointestinal major bleeding by 40% (HR 0.60; P<0.001).¹

Simple once-daily dosing

One 40 mg tablet once daily, with or without food. No dose adjustment for age, body weight, or renal impairment (CrCl ≥30 mL/min). A simplified regimen designed to support adherence and reduce prescribing complexity.³

The bleeding dilemma in atrial fibrillation

Atrial fibrillation affects over 1.4 million people in the UK and is a leading cause of ischaemic stroke.⁴ While direct oral anticoagulants have transformed stroke prevention, major bleeding remains the principal safety concern and the most common reason for treatment discontinuation or dose reduction in clinical practice. Factor XIa inhibition offers a fundamentally different approach: targeting pathological thrombosis through the intrinsic coagulation pathway while preserving haemostatic capacity.⁴ ⁵

Separating thrombosis from haemostasis

Factor XIa amplifies pathological thrombus formation through the intrinsic coagulation pathway, but plays a limited role in haemostasis at sites of vascular injury. By selectively inhibiting Factor XIa, VELOX provides potent antithrombotic protection while preserving the extrinsic pathway that maintains normal blood clotting.³ ⁵

Prescribing Information

VELOX (veloxaban) 40 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Prevention of stroke and systemic embolism in adult patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation with one or more risk factors for stroke (prior stroke/TIA/SE, age ≥75 years, hypertension, diabetes mellitus, heart failure NYHA ≥II). Dosage and administration: 40 mg once daily, with or without food. Swallow whole; do not crush, split, or chew. No dose adjustment required for age, body weight, or renal impairment (CrCl ≥30 mL/min). Contraindicated in CrCl <15 mL/min. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to veloxaban or excipients; active clinically significant bleeding; hepatic disease associated with coagulopathy (Child-Pugh C); concomitant treatment with strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors; lesion or condition at significant risk of major bleeding; prosthetic heart valve requiring anticoagulant treatment. Warnings and precautions: Assess renal function before initiation and when clinically indicated during treatment. Discontinue if severe hepatic impairment develops. If switching from a vitamin K antagonist, discontinue VKA and start VELOX when INR <2.0. Reversal: No specific antidote; andexanet alfa is not expected to reverse veloxaban. Manage major bleeding with standard supportive measures and consider prothrombin complex concentrate. Interactions: Avoid strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, ritonavir). Strong CYP3A4/P-gp inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin) may reduce efficacy; avoid concomitant use. Side effects: Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): epistaxis, contusion, gingival bleeding, haematuria. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): intracranial haemorrhage, gastrointestinal haemorrhage, ALT increase. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 28 tablets (40 mg): £58.80; 56 tablets (40 mg): £117.60. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0721.
