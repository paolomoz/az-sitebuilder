Convenient 3-weekly dosing

REVANTHA is administered as a 1200 mg intravenous infusion every 3 weeks — a single bispecific agent delivering dual checkpoint blockade in one treatment session.1

Dosing regimen

REVANTHA offers a fixed-dose regimen with no requirement for weight-based dosing, simplifying preparation and reducing waste. The first infusion is administered over 60 minutes; if well tolerated, subsequent infusions may be reduced to 30 minutes.1

First-line combination with chemotherapy

Induction (Cycles 1–4): REVANTHA 1200 mg IV every 3 weeks in combination with investigator’s choice of platinum-based chemotherapy.1

Maintenance (Cycle 5 onwards): REVANTHA 1200 mg IV monotherapy every 3 weeks until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.1

Chemotherapy doses should be administered per local prescribing information. REVANTHA should be administered first when given on the same day as chemotherapy.1

Second-line monotherapy

REVANTHA 1200 mg IV every 3 weeks as monotherapy until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity.1

No prior immunotherapy is required. REVANTHA monotherapy is indicated for patients with PD-L1 TPS ≥50% whose disease has progressed on or after platinum-based chemotherapy.1

The 30-minute infusion option (after the first well-tolerated 60-minute infusion) minimises chair time for patients receiving monotherapy.1

Preparation and administration

REVANTHA must be diluted before administration. Withdraw 60 mL from the vial and dilute in a 250 mL infusion bag of sodium chloride 9 mg/mL (0.9%) or glucose 50 mg/mL (5%).1

Gently invert the bag to mix. Do not shake. The diluted solution is stable for up to 24 hours at 2–8°C or 8 hours at room temperature (up to 25°C).1

Administer through an IV line with a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein-binding in-line filter (0.2–0.22 micron pore size). Do not co-administer other medicinal products through the same infusion line.1

Prescribing Information

REVANTHA (revantholimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

REVANTHA 20 mg/mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 1200 mg revantholimab in 60 mL.

Indication: First-line treatment of adult patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥1%) with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations, in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy. Monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC after prior platinum-based chemotherapy, whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%).

Posology: 1200 mg intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. First infusion over 60 minutes; subsequent infusions may be reduced to 30 minutes if tolerated.

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Pregnancy.
