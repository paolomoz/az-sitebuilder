Dosing and administration

NOVATREL™ (telvisoran) is administered as an intravenous infusion, with flexible dosing schedules to support individual patient needs.³

Two flexible dosing schedules

NOVATREL is available as two dosing options to accommodate different clinical settings and patient preferences:³

Standard dosing: 200 mg administered as an intravenous infusion over 30 minutes every 3 weeks (Q3W).

Extended dosing: 400 mg administered as an intravenous infusion over 30 minutes every 6 weeks (Q6W).

Both schedules deliver equivalent drug exposure. The choice of schedule should be based on clinical judgement and patient preference.³

Administration and preparation

NOVATREL should be administered by a healthcare professional experienced in cancer treatment, in a setting with appropriate resuscitation facilities.³

Preparation: Dilute in sodium chloride 9 mg/mL (0.9%) solution for injection or glucose 50 mg/mL (5%) solution for injection. Final concentration should be between 1 mg/mL and 10 mg/mL. Mix by gentle inversion — do not shake.³

Infusion: Administer through an intravenous line with a sterile, non-pyrogenic, low-protein-binding, 0.2–5 μm in-line filter over 30 minutes.³

Duration of treatment: Continue until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or a maximum of 24 months in patients without disease progression.³

Dose modifications for immune-mediated adverse reactions

No dose reductions of NOVATREL are recommended. Manage immune-mediated adverse reactions through treatment interruption or permanent discontinuation. Withhold NOVATREL for Grade 2 immune-mediated adverse reactions (except endocrinopathies) and initiate corticosteroids (1–2 mg/kg/day prednisone or equivalent). Resume when adverse reactions improve to Grade ≤1 and corticosteroids have been tapered. Permanently discontinue for Grade 3 or 4 immune-mediated adverse reactions, recurrent Grade 2 reactions after resumption, or inability to reduce corticosteroids to ≤10 mg/day prednisone within 12 weeks.³

Prescribing Information

NOVATREL (telvisoran) 200 mg/10 mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: First-line monotherapy for adults with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%), with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations. Dosage: 200 mg by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes every 3 weeks, or 400 mg every 6 weeks. Treat until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 24 months. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to telvisoran or any excipient. Warnings and precautions: Immune-mediated adverse reactions may be severe or fatal. Monitor for signs and symptoms of pneumonitis, hepatitis, colitis, endocrinopathies, nephritis, and skin reactions. Manage with dose interruption, corticosteroids, or permanent discontinuation as appropriate. Infusion-related reactions have been reported. Pregnancy and lactation: May cause foetal harm; effective contraception required during treatment and for 4 months after. Breastfeeding not recommended during treatment and for 4 months after. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, decreased appetite, rash, diarrhoea, nausea. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): cough, pruritus, dyspnoea, constipation, pneumonitis, hypothyroidism. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 1 vial (200 mg/10 mL): £2,630.00.
