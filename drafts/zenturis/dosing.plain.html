Dosing and administration

Zenturigliptin is taken as a single 14 mg tablet once weekly — the first oral GLP-1 receptor agonist that does not require injection.4

How to take Zenturis

Zenturigliptin 14 mg should be taken once weekly, on the same day each week, in the morning on an empty stomach.4

The tablet should be swallowed whole with a sip of plain water (up to 120 mL). Patients should not eat, drink, or take other oral medicinal products for at least 30 minutes after taking zenturigliptin.4

The tablet must not be crushed, chewed, or split, as this may affect absorption.4

Dosing schedule and missed doses

Fixed dose: Zenturigliptin is prescribed at a fixed dose of 14 mg once weekly. No dose titration is required.4

Missed dose: If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as possible, provided there are at least 3 days (72 hours) before the next scheduled dose. If fewer than 3 days remain, the missed dose should be skipped and the next dose taken on the regularly scheduled day.4

Day change: The day of weekly administration may be changed, provided there are at least 3 days between two doses.4

Use with other diabetes medicines

Zenturigliptin can be used as monotherapy (when metformin is not tolerated) or in combination with metformin, sulfonylureas, SGLT2 inhibitors, or basal insulin. When combined with a sulfonylurea or insulin, a dose reduction of the sulfonylurea or insulin may be required to reduce the risk of hypoglycaemia. Zenturigliptin should not be used in combination with other GLP-1 receptor agonists or DPP-4 inhibitors.4

Prescribing Information

ZENTURIS (zenturigliptin) 14 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

Indication: Treatment of adults with insufficiently controlled type 2 diabetes mellitus as an adjunct to diet and exercise.

Dosage and administration: 14 mg once weekly, taken in the morning on an empty stomach with a sip of plain water. The tablet must be swallowed whole and not crushed, chewed, or split. No food, drink, or other oral medicinal products should be taken for at least 30 minutes after administration.

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to zenturigliptin or any excipient. Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2.

Warnings and precautions: Not recommended in patients with severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²). Monitor liver function tests prior to initiation and periodically thereafter. Not a substitute for insulin. Pancreatitis: discontinue if suspected.

Pregnancy and lactation: Not recommended.

Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): nausea, diarrhoea. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): vomiting, decreased appetite, constipation, abdominal pain, ALT increase, headache.

Legal category: POM.

Pack and price: 4 tablets (28-day supply): £185.64.

Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0486.
