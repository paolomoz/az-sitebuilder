Prescribing Information

VELOX (veloxaban) 40 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Prevention of stroke and systemic embolism in adult patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation with one or more risk factors for stroke (prior stroke/TIA/SE, age ≥75 years, hypertension, diabetes mellitus, heart failure NYHA ≥II). Dosage and administration: 40 mg once daily, with or without food. Swallow whole; do not crush, split, or chew. No dose adjustment required for age, body weight, or renal impairment (CrCl ≥30 mL/min). Contraindicated in CrCl <15 mL/min. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to veloxaban or excipients; active clinically significant bleeding; hepatic disease associated with coagulopathy (Child-Pugh C); concomitant treatment with strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors; lesion or condition at significant risk of major bleeding; prosthetic heart valve requiring anticoagulant treatment. Warnings and precautions: Assess renal function before initiation and when clinically indicated during treatment. Discontinue if severe hepatic impairment develops. If switching from a vitamin K antagonist, discontinue VKA and start VELOX when INR <2.0. Reversal: No specific antidote; andexanet alfa is not expected to reverse veloxaban. Manage major bleeding with standard supportive measures and consider prothrombin complex concentrate. Interactions: Avoid strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, ritonavir). Strong CYP3A4/P-gp inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin) may reduce efficacy; avoid concomitant use. Side effects: Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): epistaxis, contusion, gingival bleeding, haematuria. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): intracranial haemorrhage, gastrointestinal haemorrhage, ALT increase. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 28 tablets (40 mg): £58.80; 56 tablets (40 mg): £117.60. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0721.
