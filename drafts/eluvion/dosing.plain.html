Simple three-weekly intravenous infusion

ELUVION 200 mg is administered as a 60-minute intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. Fixed dose — no weight-based calculations. No loading dose required.1

Step 1: Assess eligibility

Confirm histologically verified advanced clear cell renal cell carcinoma. Verify adequate organ function: hepatic (ALT/AST ≤3× ULN, total bilirubin ≤1.5× ULN), renal (CrCl ≥30 mL/min), and haematological (haemoglobin ≥9 g/dL, platelets ≥100 × 109/L). Optimise blood pressure control before initiation. Obtain baseline urinalysis for proteinuria assessment.1

Step 2: Initiate treatment

Administer ELUVION 200 mg as a 60-minute intravenous infusion every 3 weeks (21-day cycle). Dilute the required volume of ELUVION concentrate in 250 mL sodium chloride 9 mg/mL (0.9%) solution for infusion. The diluted solution may be stored at room temperature (up to 25°C) for a maximum of 6 hours or refrigerated (2–8°C) for up to 24 hours from the time of preparation. Administer using an infusion set with a 0.2 μm in-line filter.1

Step 3: Monitor and follow up

Monitor blood pressure at each visit. Assess liver function tests every 3 weeks for the first 6 months, then periodically. Monitor urine protein periodically. Continue treatment until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Dose modifications are not recommended; manage adverse events by withholding or permanently discontinuing treatment as per the SmPC guidance.1

Designed for straightforward clinical use

ELUVION uses a fixed 200 mg dose regardless of body weight, eliminating the need for weight-based calculations. The three-weekly infusion schedule aligns with standard oncology follow-up intervals, minimising additional clinic visits for patients.1

Storage and handling

Store unopened vials refrigerated at 2–8°C in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze. Do not shake.1

The diluted solution for infusion may be stored at room temperature (up to 25°C) for a maximum of 6 hours, including infusion time, or refrigerated at 2–8°C for up to 24 hours from the time of preparation. If refrigerated, allow the diluted solution to reach room temperature before administration.1

Before dilution, allow the vial to reach room temperature for approximately 15 minutes. Inspect visually: the concentrate should be a clear to slightly opalescent, colourless to pale yellow solution, free from visible particles. Discard if discoloured or if particulate matter is observed.1

Prescribing Information

ELUVION (eluvionimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. ELUVION 200 mg concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 200 mg eluvionimab in 10 mL (20 mg/mL). Indication: Treatment of adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) with clear cell histology, as monotherapy or in combination with standard of care, who have not received prior systemic therapy for advanced disease. Posology: 200 mg administered as an intravenous infusion over 60 minutes every 3 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.

Adverse Event Reporting

Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store. Adverse events should also be reported to AstraZeneca by visiting contactazmedical.astrazeneca.com or by calling 0800 783 0033.
