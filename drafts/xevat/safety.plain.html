Safety profile of XEVAT

The safety of XEVAT was evaluated in 1,287 patients with rheumatoid arthritis across the RESOLVE clinical programme, with a median treatment duration of 11.2 months.3 4

Well-characterised safety profile

The overall safety profile of XEVAT was consistent with the known class effects of JAK inhibitors. The rate of serious adverse events was comparable between treatment groups (8.4% vs 7.9%).3 4

Important safety considerations

Infections: serious infections occurred in 2.8% of patients. Do not initiate XEVAT during active serious infection; interrupt if serious infection develops.1

Herpes zoster: increased risk (3.8% vs 0.9%) — consider vaccination prior to starting treatment.1

Haematological monitoring: monitor neutrophil count, lymphocyte count, and haemoglobin at baseline and during treatment. Do not initiate if ANC <1,000/mm³, ALC <500/mm³, or Hb <8 g/dL.1

Lipids: monitor 12 weeks after initiation; manage hyperlipidaemia per clinical guidelines.1

Pregnancy & breastfeeding

Contraindicated in pregnancy. Effective contraception required during treatment and for 1 week after the last dose. Not recommended during breastfeeding.1

Renal & hepatic impairment

Reduce to 100 mg once daily in moderate renal impairment. Not recommended in severe renal or severe hepatic impairment.1

Elderly patients

No dose adjustment required for patients ≥65 years; no clinically meaningful differences in efficacy observed.1

Prescribing Information

XEVAT (xevatirel) 100 mg and 200 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

Indication: Treatment of moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis in adults with inadequate response or intolerance to one or more DMARDs; treatment of active psoriatic arthritis in adults with inadequate response or intolerance to a prior DMARD therapy.

Dosage: 200 mg once daily; reduce to 100 mg once daily for moderate renal impairment or with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors.

Contraindications: Severe hepatic impairment; severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min); pregnancy; active serious infection.

Warnings and precautions: Serious infections, herpes zoster, haematological abnormalities, lipid elevations — see full SmPC.

Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): upper respiratory tract infection. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): nausea, headache, urinary tract infection, elevated CPK, herpes zoster, neutropenia.

Legal category: POM. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd, 2 Pancras Square, London N1C 4AG.
