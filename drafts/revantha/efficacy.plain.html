Proven survival benefit across the ASCEND programme

REVANTHA demonstrated statistically significant improvements in progression-free survival and overall survival across two pivotal Phase III trials in metastatic NSCLC.1 2

The ASCEND clinical programme

The ASCEND programme evaluated the efficacy and safety of REVANTHA in over 1,800 patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer across two pivotal Phase III randomised, controlled trials.1 2

ASCEND-1: First-line treatment with chemotherapy

In ASCEND-1, patients treated with REVANTHA + chemotherapy achieved a median overall survival of 22.1 months, compared with 14.9 months with placebo + chemotherapy (HR 0.64, 95% CI: 0.53–0.77; P<0.001).1

Median PFS was 9.8 months vs 5.4 months (HR 0.56, P<0.001), with an objective response rate of 52.3% vs 33.1%.1

ASCEND-2: Monotherapy after prior chemotherapy

In ASCEND-2, REVANTHA monotherapy achieved a median overall survival of 18.7 months vs 10.3 months with docetaxel (HR 0.58, 95% CI: 0.46–0.73; P<0.001).2

Patients treated with REVANTHA also demonstrated a higher objective response rate (38.6% vs 14.2%) and longer median duration of response (16.1 months vs 6.2 months).2

Prescribing Information

REVANTHA (revantholimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

REVANTHA 20 mg/mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 1200 mg revantholimab in 60 mL.

Indication: First-line treatment of adult patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥1%) with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations, in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy. Monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC after prior platinum-based chemotherapy, whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%).

Posology: 1200 mg intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. First infusion over 60 minutes; subsequent infusions may be reduced to 30 minutes if tolerated.

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Pregnancy.
