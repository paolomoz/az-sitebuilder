ELUVION (eluvionimab)

A first-in-class selective HIF-2α inhibitor for the treatment of adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma.1

Explore the HORIZON data

42% improvement in progression-free survival

In HORIZON-1, ELUVION demonstrated a median PFS of 14.2 months compared with 8.4 months for sunitinib in previously untreated advanced RCC (HR 0.59; 95% CI: 0.48–0.73; P<0.001).1

A well-characterised safety profile

Serious adverse events occurred in 22.4% of ELUVION-treated patients compared with 28.1% in the sunitinib group. Treatment discontinuation due to adverse events was 8.2%.1

Simple three-weekly intravenous infusion

ELUVION 200 mg is administered as a 60-minute intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. No loading dose required. No weight-based dosing.1

Advanced renal cell carcinoma: a persistent challenge in oncology

Renal cell carcinoma accounts for approximately 13,300 new diagnoses and 4,600 deaths annually in the UK.2 Despite advances in targeted therapy and immunotherapy, approximately 30% of patients with advanced RCC experience disease progression within 12 months of first-line treatment, underscoring the need for therapies that address the underlying biology of clear cell RCC.3

Targeting HIF-2α at the source of tumour angiogenesis

In clear cell RCC, loss of VHL function leads to constitutive HIF-2α accumulation, driving VEGF-mediated angiogenesis and tumour proliferation. By selectively inhibiting HIF-2α, ELUVION suppresses tumour vascularisation at its transcriptional origin — upstream of VEGF, PDGF, and other pro-angiogenic mediators.1

Prescribing Information

ELUVION (eluvionimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. ELUVION 200 mg concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 200 mg eluvionimab in 10 mL (20 mg/mL). Indication: Treatment of adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) with clear cell histology, as monotherapy or in combination with standard of care, who have not received prior systemic therapy for advanced disease. Posology: 200 mg administered as an intravenous infusion over 60 minutes every 3 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.

Adverse Event Reporting

Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store.
