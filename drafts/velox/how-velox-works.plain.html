How VELOX works

VELOX® (veloxaban) is a first-in-class selective oral Factor XIa inhibitor that separates antithrombotic efficacy from haemostatic impairment — a new approach to anticoagulation.³ ⁵

The coagulation cascade: two pathways, one clinical dilemma

Current anticoagulants (Factor Xa and direct thrombin inhibitors) target components shared by both pathological thrombosis and physiological haemostasis, inherently coupling antithrombotic efficacy to bleeding risk. The intrinsic coagulation pathway — amplified by Factor XIa — contributes disproportionately to pathological thrombus formation (as seen in AF, VTE, and arterial thrombosis) while playing a limited role in the haemostatic response to vascular injury. Clinical genetic evidence confirms this: inherited Factor XI deficiency protects against thrombotic events with only mild bleeding tendency.⁴ ⁵

Selective Factor XIa inhibition: targeting the amplification loop

Veloxaban binds selectively to the active site of Factor XIa with high affinity (Ki = 0.2 nM), suppressing thrombin amplification through the intrinsic pathway without affecting Factor Xa, thrombin, or other serine proteases in the coagulation cascade. This selective inhibition reduces fibrin-rich thrombus propagation in the low-shear environment of the left atrial appendage — the primary site of thrombus formation in AF — and attenuates Factor XIa-driven platelet activation.³ ⁵

Critically, veloxaban preserves the extrinsic (tissue factor) pathway, which is responsible for initiating haemostasis at sites of vascular injury. This mechanistic separation is the pharmacological basis for the reduced bleeding observed across the VELOCITY programme, including the clinically important categories of intracranial haemorrhage and gastrointestinal bleeding.³ ⁵

Pharmacological profile

Oral bioavailability: ~72%, not significantly affected by food. Time to peak plasma concentration (Tmax): 2–3 hours. Elimination half-life: 10–14 hours, supporting once-daily dosing. Metabolism: Primarily hepatic via CYP3A4, with minor renal excretion (~25% of administered dose). Protein binding: 92%. No clinically significant effect on PT, aPTT, or anti-Factor Xa assays at therapeutic concentrations, minimising interference with routine coagulation monitoring.³

Prescribing Information

VELOX (veloxaban) 40 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Prevention of stroke and systemic embolism in adult patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation with one or more risk factors for stroke (prior stroke/TIA/SE, age ≥75 years, hypertension, diabetes mellitus, heart failure NYHA ≥II). Dosage and administration: 40 mg once daily, with or without food. Swallow whole; do not crush, split, or chew. No dose adjustment required for age, body weight, or renal impairment (CrCl ≥30 mL/min). Contraindicated in CrCl <15 mL/min. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to veloxaban or excipients; active clinically significant bleeding; hepatic disease associated with coagulopathy (Child-Pugh C); concomitant treatment with strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors; lesion or condition at significant risk of major bleeding; prosthetic heart valve requiring anticoagulant treatment. Warnings and precautions: Assess renal function before initiation and when clinically indicated during treatment. Discontinue if severe hepatic impairment develops. If switching from a vitamin K antagonist, discontinue VKA and start VELOX when INR <2.0. Reversal: No specific antidote; andexanet alfa is not expected to reverse veloxaban. Manage major bleeding with standard supportive measures and consider prothrombin complex concentrate. Interactions: Avoid strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, ritonavir). Strong CYP3A4/P-gp inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin) may reduce efficacy; avoid concomitant use. Side effects: Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): epistaxis, contusion, gingival bleeding, haematuria. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): intracranial haemorrhage, gastrointestinal haemorrhage, ALT increase. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 28 tablets (40 mg): £58.80; 56 tablets (40 mg): £117.60. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0721.
