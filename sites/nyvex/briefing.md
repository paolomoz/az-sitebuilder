## 2. Mechanism of Action

NYVEX (nyvexabart) is a fully human IgG1 monoclonal antibody that selectively binds to the interleukin-31 receptor alpha subunit (IL-31Ra), blocking IL-31 signalling.^1

### The neuroimmune itch cycle in prurigo nodularis

Prurigo nodularis is driven by a self-reinforcing neuroimmune feedback loop. IL-31, produced predominantly by activated Th2 cells, acts on IL-31Ra expressed on sensory neurons and keratinocytes. This signalling drives the intense, chronic pruritus that defines PN. Persistent scratching in response to pruritus leads to nodule formation, which further amplifies the itch-scratch cycle.^1,2

### How NYVEX interrupts the cycle

- Selectively blocks IL-31Ra on sensory neurons, interrupting the neural itch signal^1
- Reduces keratinocyte-mediated inflammatory signalling downstream of IL-31^1
- Breaks the itch-scratch-nodule feedback loop that perpetuates disease^1,2
- Does not result in broad immunosuppression — selectivity for IL-31Ra avoids the off-target effects associated with dual IL-4Ra blockade^1

### Differentiation

Unlike agents that target the IL-4/IL-13 pathway, NYVEX selectively neutralises IL-31 signalling. Conjunctivitis, a recognised class effect of IL-4/IL-13 pathway inhibitors, was not observed at increased rates with NYVEX in clinical trials — consistent with its selective mechanism of action.^1

## 3. Clinical Programme

### RESOLVE Phase III Programme

The NYVEX approval is supported by the **RESOLVE programme** — two Phase III, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials conducted in adults with moderate-to-severe prurigo nodularis.^3,4

### RESOLVE-1 and RESOLVE-2 (pooled analysis)

- **Design**: Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled
- **Population**: 612 adults with confirmed moderate-to-severe PN (IGA-PN >= 3, minimum 20 nodules)
- **Duration**: 24 weeks
- **Primary endpoint**: Proportion of patients achieving IGA-PN 0/1 (clear or almost clear) at Week 24
- **Key secondary endpoints**: WI-NRS itch reduction, DLQI, nodule count reduction, sleep disturbance improvement
- **Publication**: [PLACEHOLDER — requires reference details]

### Primary endpoint

| Endpoint | NYVEX | Placebo | P-value |
|----------|-------|---------|---------|
| IGA-PN 0/1 (clear or almost clear) at Week 24 | 58.3% | 16.1% | p < 0.0001 |

### Key secondary endpoints (Week 24)

| Endpoint | NYVEX | Placebo |
|----------|-------|---------|
| >= 4-point reduction in WI-NRS (worst itch) | 67.4% | 20.8% |
| DLQI 0/1 (no impact on quality of life) | 44.1% | 11.3% |
| Reduction in active nodule count >= 75% | 61.8% | 18.4% |
| Sleep disturbance NRS improvement >= 3 points | 55.2% | 17.9% |

### Onset of action

A meaningful reduction in WI-NRS (>= 4 points) was observed as early as **Week 2** in a proportion of patients, with separation from placebo reaching statistical significance from **Week 4** onward.^3,4

#### Section 3 — Administration details

Item 1 — Injection site guidance:
- NYVEX should be injected subcutaneously into the thigh, abdomen (avoiding the 5 cm area around the navel), or upper arm (if administered by a caregiver). Injection sites should be rotated with each dose. Do not inject into areas where the skin is tender, bruised, red, or hard.^1

Item 2 — Storage conditions:
- Store in a refrigerator (2-8 degrees C). Do not freeze. Keep in the outer carton to protect from light. Prior to use, NYVEX may be kept at room temperature (up to 25 degrees C) for a maximum of 14 days. [PLACEHOLDER — confirm storage details from SmPC]

Item 3 — Preparation steps:
- Remove the autoinjector from the refrigerator and allow to reach room temperature for approximately 30 minutes before use. Do not warm by any other means. Inspect visually — the solution should be clear to slightly opalescent, colourless to pale yellow. Do not use if the solution is cloudy, discoloured, or contains particles. [PLACEHOLDER — confirm from IFU]
