Dosing and monitoring

SERANOVA™ (valinectra) uses a simple potassium-guided titration to optimise cardiorenal protection while managing the risk of hyperkalaemia.3

Potassium-guided dose titration

Step 1 — Before initiation: Measure serum potassium. Do not initiate if K⁺ >5.0 mmol/L or eGFR <25 mL/min/1.73m².3

Step 2 — Initiate: Start SERANOVA 10 mg once daily.3

Step 3 — Check at 4 weeks: Measure serum potassium.3

Step 4 — Titrate based on potassium: K⁺ ≤4.8 mmol/L: Uptitrate to 20 mg once daily. K⁺ >4.8 to 5.0 mmol/L: Maintain 10 mg once daily. K⁺ >5.0 to 5.5 mmol/L: Maintain current dose; recheck in 4 weeks. K⁺ >5.5 mmol/L: Withhold SERANOVA; recheck; resume 10 mg when K⁺ ≤5.0 mmol/L.3

Administration and practical guidance

SERANOVA should be taken once daily, with or without food. Tablets should be swallowed whole with water.3

If a dose is missed, take it as soon as remembered on the same day. Do not take two doses on the same day to make up for a missed dose.3

SERANOVA may be prescribed alongside existing guideline-directed therapy including ACE inhibitors or ARBs, SGLT2 inhibitors, and GLP-1 receptor agonists. In the RENOVA programme, 97% of patients received concomitant RAAS inhibition, 59% received SGLT2 inhibitors, and 12% received GLP-1 RAs.1

Do not use with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (contraindicated) or with other potassium-sparing diuretics (use with caution).3

Monitoring recommendations

Serum potassium: Measure before initiation, within 4 weeks of starting or dose change, and periodically thereafter. Additional monitoring is recommended during intercurrent illness, changes in concomitant medications affecting potassium, or changes in renal function.3

Renal function: Measure eGFR at baseline and periodically. An initial eGFR decline of 2–4 mL/min/1.73m² may occur within the first 4 weeks; this is typically haemodynamic and reversible. Continue treatment unless eGFR declines below 25 mL/min/1.73m² on two consecutive measurements.3

Hepatic function: Monitor liver function tests periodically during treatment.3

Prescribing Information

SERANOVA (valinectra) 10 mg and 20 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Reduction of sustained eGFR decline, end-stage kidney disease, cardiovascular death, and hospitalisation for heart failure in adults with chronic kidney disease associated with type 2 diabetes mellitus. Dosage and administration: Initiate at 10 mg once daily. Measure serum potassium before initiation and within 4 weeks. If serum potassium ≤4.8 mmol/L, uptitrate to 20 mg once daily. If serum potassium >4.8 to 5.0 mmol/L, maintain 10 mg. If serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L, withhold and recheck. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to valinectra or any excipient. Concomitant use with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g. itraconazole, ketoconazole, ritonavir, clarithromycin). Addison’s disease. Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C). Warnings and precautions: Monitor serum potassium before initiation, at 4 weeks, and periodically thereafter. Interrupt treatment if potassium >5.5 mmol/L. Do not initiate if serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L or eGFR <25 mL/min/1.73m². Use with caution in patients receiving potassium-sparing diuretics or potassium supplements. An initial eGFR decline may occur upon treatment initiation; this is typically reversible and not a reason to discontinue. Interactions: Contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors. Avoid grapefruit juice. Moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors: no dose adjustment required but monitor potassium more frequently. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): none. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): hyperkalaemia, hypotension. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): dizziness, pruritus, hyponatraemia. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 28 tablets (10 mg): £524.60; 28 tablets (20 mg): £524.60. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0527.
