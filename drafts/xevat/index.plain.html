XEVAT (xevatirel)

Selective oral JAK1 inhibitor for the treatment of moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis in adults with inadequate response to DMARDs.1

Rheumatoid arthritis — unmet need persists

Despite the availability of biologic DMARDs, approximately 40% of patients with RA fail to achieve low disease activity with current therapies, and many experience loss of response over time.2 5 There remains a significant need for effective, convenient oral therapies that provide rapid and sustained disease control.

Selective JAK1 inhibition

Prescribing Information

XEVAT (xevatirel) 100 mg and 200 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

Indication: Treatment of moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis in adults with inadequate response or intolerance to one or more DMARDs; treatment of active psoriatic arthritis in adults with inadequate response or intolerance to a prior DMARD therapy.

Dosage: 200 mg once daily; reduce to 100 mg once daily for moderate renal impairment or with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors.

Contraindications: Severe hepatic impairment; severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min); pregnancy; active serious infection.

Warnings and precautions: Serious infections, herpes zoster, haematological abnormalities, lipid elevations — see full SmPC.

Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): upper respiratory tract infection. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): nausea, headache, urinary tract infection, elevated CPK, herpes zoster, neutropenia.

Legal category: POM. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd, 2 Pancras Square, London N1C 4AG.
