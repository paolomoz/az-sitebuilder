Safety profile of VITESSA

The safety of VITESSA was evaluated in 989 patients with severe asthma across the CLARITY programme, with a median exposure of 11.8 months.¹ ²

A favourable safety profile consistent with upstream IL-33 blockade

Serious adverse events were less frequent with VITESSA than with placebo (8.2% vs 12.1%), driven by fewer asthma-related hospitalisations. Treatment discontinuation due to adverse events was low (2.1%), and the overall infection rate was comparable between groups.¹ ²

Injection site reactions

The most frequently reported adverse event (12.4%), typically mild (Grade 1), localised erythema or pain lasting less than 24 hours. No injection site reactions led to treatment discontinuation.¹

Herpes zoster and vaccination

Reported in 2.8% of VITESSA-treated patients versus 0.4% with placebo. Consider varicella zoster vaccination before initiating treatment in eligible patients.¹

Helminth infection screening

IL-33 contributes to anti-helminth immunity. Screen for and treat pre-existing helminth infections before starting VITESSA. Consider temporarily withholding if infections develop during treatment.¹

Special populations

Pregnancy: No adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. Animal reproductive toxicology studies did not reveal direct harmful effects. VITESSA should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the foetus.¹

Elderly patients: No dose adjustment is required in patients aged ≥65 years. Clinical trial experience in patients aged ≥75 years is limited; no additional safety signals were identified.¹

Adolescents: VITESSA is currently under investigation in the CLARITY-3 trial for patients aged 12–17 years. It is not yet approved for use in adolescents.¹

Renal and hepatic impairment: No dose adjustment is required for mild-to-moderate renal or hepatic impairment. VITESSA has not been studied in severe renal or hepatic impairment.¹

Prescribing Information

VITESSA (vitessamab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. VITESSA 210 mg solution for injection in a pre-filled pen. Each pen contains 210 mg vitessamab in 1.4 mL. Indication: Add-on maintenance treatment in adults with severe eosinophilic asthma inadequately controlled despite high-dose ICS plus a LABA. Posology: 210 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.

Adverse Event Reporting

Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store. Adverse events should also be reported to AstraZeneca by visiting contactazmedical.astrazeneca.com or by calling 0800 783 0033.
