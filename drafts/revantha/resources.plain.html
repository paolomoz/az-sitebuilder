Prescribing Information

REVANTHA (revantholimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

REVANTHA 20 mg/mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 1200 mg revantholimab in 60 mL.

Indication: First-line treatment of adult patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥1%) with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations, in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy. Monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC after prior platinum-based chemotherapy, whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%).

Posology: 1200 mg intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. First infusion over 60 minutes; subsequent infusions may be reduced to 30 minutes if tolerated.

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Pregnancy.

Adverse Event Reporting

Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store.

Adverse events should also be reported to AstraZeneca by visiting contactazmedical.astrazeneca.com or by calling 0800 783 0033.
