# 2. Mechanism of Action

Trelimovab is a human immunoglobulin G2λ monoclonal antibody that specifically binds to thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP), an epithelial cytokine released at the top of the inflammatory cascade in response to airway insults including allergens, viruses, pollutants, and other triggers.

TSLP is a key upstream initiator of airway inflammation in asthma. By binding directly to TSLP and preventing its interaction with the TSLP receptor complex (TSLPR/IL-7Rα), trelimovab inhibits multiple downstream inflammatory pathways:

- Eosinophilic inflammation via suppression of IL-5, IL-13, and eosinophil recruitment
- Type 2 (Th2) immune responses including IgE production and mast cell activation
- Non-type 2 inflammation including neutrophilic airway inflammation, mast cell cytokine release, and structural cell activation
- Airway remodelling through reduced subepithelial fibrosis and smooth muscle hypertrophy

Because TSLP acts upstream of multiple inflammatory pathways, trelimovab provides broad anti-inflammatory activity across the full spectrum of severe asthma phenotypes --- including patients with low eosinophil counts and those without a clearly defined type 2 inflammatory biomarker signature.

## Injection site reactions

Injection site reactions (ISRs) occurred in 4.6% of TRELUXIA-treated patients vs 2.8% with placebo. The most common ISRs were erythema, pain, and swelling at the injection site. All ISRs were mild to moderate in severity. No ISR led to treatment discontinuation.³

## Key safety findings

**Serious adverse events:** 8.4% TRELUXIA vs 11.2% placebo (driven by fewer asthma-related hospitalisations)

**Treatment discontinuation due to AEs:** 2.4% vs 3.1%

**All-cause mortality:** 0.1% vs 0.2% (no drug-related deaths)

**Anaphylaxis:** 3 events (0.4%) in the TRELUXIA group vs 0 in placebo. All events occurred within 24 hours of injection and resolved with standard treatment. Patients should be observed for an appropriate period after injection, particularly after the first 3 doses.³

**Malignancy:** 0.3% vs 0.4% (no imbalance; no signal for increased malignancy risk)

**Helminth infections:** 0.4% vs 0.1%. Treat pre-existing helminth infections before initiating TRELUXIA. If patients become infected during treatment and do not respond to anti-helminth therapy, discontinue TRELUXIA until infection resolves.³

**Immunogenicity:** Treatment-emergent anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) were detected in 5.1% of TRELUXIA-treated patients. Neutralising antibodies were detected in 1.2%. ADAs had no clinically meaningful impact on pharmacokinetics, efficacy, or safety.³

## Special populations

- **Adolescents (12--17 years):** Same dose as adults (210 mg Q4W). In LUMINOS-1, 63 adolescents received TRELUXIA; efficacy and safety were consistent with the overall adult population.³

- **Elderly (≥65 years):** No dose adjustment required. Limited data in patients ≥75 years. 11% of LUMINOS-1 patients were ≥65 years.³

- **Renal and hepatic impairment:** No dose adjustment for mild to moderate renal or hepatic impairment. TRELUXIA has not been studied in severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²) or severe hepatic impairment.³

- **Pregnancy and lactation:** There are no adequate data on the use of trelimovab in pregnant women. Human IgG antibodies are known to cross the placental barrier; trelimovab may be transmitted from mother to foetus. TRELUXIA should only be used during pregnancy if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the foetus. It is not known whether trelimovab is excreted in human milk. A decision must be made whether to discontinue breastfeeding or discontinue therapy.³

# 5. Prescribing Information (abbreviated --- for every page footer)

TRELUXIA (trelimovab) 210 mg/1.91 mL solution for injection in pre-filled syringe and pre-filled pen. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Add-on maintenance treatment for adults and adolescents aged ≥12 years with severe asthma that is inadequately controlled despite high-dose inhaled corticosteroids plus at least one additional controller, irrespective of inflammatory phenotype. Dosage and administration: 210 mg administered by subcutaneous injection once every 4 weeks. Administer into the thigh, abdomen (excluding 5 cm around the navel), or upper arm (if administered by a caregiver). Rotate injection sites. Do not inject into areas that are tender, bruised, erythematous, or indurated. The first 3 doses should be administered under the supervision of a healthcare professional. Thereafter, a patient or caregiver may administer TRELUXIA after training in subcutaneous injection technique. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to trelimovab or any excipient. Warnings and precautions: Do not use to treat acute asthma symptoms or acute exacerbations. Do not abruptly discontinue systemic or inhaled corticosteroids upon initiation of TRELUXIA. Reduce corticosteroids gradually under medical supervision. Hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylaxis have been reported. If a serious hypersensitivity reaction occurs, discontinue TRELUXIA and initiate appropriate therapy. Treat pre-existing helminth infections before initiating TRELUXIA. Interactions: No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted. Population pharmacokinetic analyses indicated that commonly used asthma medications (ICS, LABA, LTRA, LAMA, OCS, theophylline) did not affect the pharmacokinetics of trelimovab. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): nasopharyngitis. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): upper respiratory tract infection, headache, injection site reactions, arthralgia. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): anaphylaxis, pharyngitis. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 1 pre-filled syringe (210 mg): £1,267.00; 1 pre-filled pen (210 mg): £1,267.00. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0643. Full prescribing information available from: AstraZeneca UK Ltd, 2 Pancras Square, London N1C 4AG. **Body text:** Adolescents (12--17 years): Same dose as adults (210 mg Q4W). In LUMINOS-1, 63 adolescents received TRELUXIA with consistent efficacy and safety.³\
\
Elderly patients: No dose adjustment required in patients aged ≥65 years. 11% of LUMINOS-1 patients were ≥65 years.³\
\

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

### Section 2 --- The TSLP pathway (two-column)

**Block:** columns-teaser

**Image:** columns-tslp-pathway.jpeg | **CDN URL:** https://treluxia-images.pages.dev/columns-tslp-pathway.jpeg

*Usage: MoA --- TSLP pathway*

**Heading:** TSLP: the upstream driver of severe asthma inflammation

**Body text:** Thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP) is produced by airway epithelial cells in response to environmental triggers including allergens, viral infections, pollutants, and mechanical injury.⁵\
\
Once released, TSLP activates dendritic cells and innate lymphoid cells (ILC2s), initiating a cascade of downstream inflammatory events: Th2 cell differentiation and IL-4/IL-5/IL-13 production, eosinophil recruitment and activation, IgE class switching and mast cell sensitisation, and structural cell activation driving airway remodelling.⁴ ⁵\
\
Crucially, TSLP also drives non-type 2 inflammatory pathways including mast cell cytokine release and neutrophil-mediated inflammation, making it relevant across the full phenotypic spectrum of severe asthma.⁵

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

### Section 3 --- TRELUXIA mechanism (two-column) \| highlight background

**Block:** columns-teaser

**Section metadata:** Style: highlight

**Image:** columns-mechanism.jpeg | **CDN URL:** https://treluxia-images.pages.dev/columns-mechanism.jpeg

*Usage: MoA --- TRELUXIA mechanism*

**Heading:** How TRELUXIA blocks TSLP signalling

**Body text:** Trelimovab is a human IgG2λ monoclonal antibody that binds specifically to TSLP, preventing its interaction with the heterodimeric TSLP receptor complex (TSLPR/IL-7Rα) on the surface of immune cells.³\
\
By neutralising TSLP at the top of the inflammatory cascade, trelimovab inhibits the activation of downstream type 2 and non-type 2 pathways simultaneously --- reducing eosinophilic inflammation, Th2 cytokine production, IgE synthesis, mast cell activation, and non-eosinophilic inflammatory mechanisms.³ ⁵\
\
This broad upstream mechanism distinguishes TRELUXIA from biologics that target single downstream mediators (IL-5, IL-4/IL-13, IgE), enabling efficacy across all severe asthma phenotypes including those with low eosinophil counts and mixed inflammatory signatures.³

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

### Section 4 --- Positioning in the treatment pathway (two-column) \| light background

**Block:** columns-teaser

**Section metadata:** Style: light

**Image:** columns-positioning.jpeg | **CDN URL:** https://treluxia-images.pages.dev/columns-positioning.jpeg

*Usage: MoA --- treatment positioning*

**Heading:** TRELUXIA in the severe asthma treatment pathway

**Body text:** Current guidelines recommend biologic therapy for patients with severe asthma that remains uncontrolled despite high-dose ICS plus additional controllers.⁴ Existing biologic options --- anti-IL-5/5R, anti-IL-4/13, and anti-IgE --- primarily target type 2 inflammatory pathways and require biomarker-based patient selection.⁴\
\
TRELUXIA's upstream TSLP blockade offers a complementary approach: it is indicated irrespective of inflammatory phenotype, providing a treatment option for patients across the entire severe asthma spectrum --- including those who may not be eligible for or who have responded inadequately to existing biologics targeting single downstream pathways.³ ⁴
