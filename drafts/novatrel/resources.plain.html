Prescribing Information

NOVATREL (telvisoran) 200 mg/10 mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: First-line monotherapy for adults with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%), with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations. Dosage: 200 mg by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes every 3 weeks, or 400 mg every 6 weeks. Treat until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 24 months. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to telvisoran or any excipient. Warnings and precautions: Immune-mediated adverse reactions may be severe or fatal. Monitor for signs and symptoms of pneumonitis, hepatitis, colitis, endocrinopathies, nephritis, and skin reactions. Manage with dose interruption, corticosteroids, or permanent discontinuation as appropriate. Infusion-related reactions have been reported. Pregnancy and lactation: May cause foetal harm; effective contraception required during treatment and for 4 months after. Breastfeeding not recommended during treatment and for 4 months after. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, decreased appetite, rash, diarrhoea, nausea. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): cough, pruritus, dyspnoea, constipation, pneumonitis, hypothyroidism. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 1 vial (200 mg/10 mL): £2,630.00. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0512. Full prescribing information available from: AstraZeneca UK Ltd, 2 Pancras Square, London N1C 4AG. Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store. Adverse events should also be reported to AstraZeneca by visiting contactazmedical.astrazeneca.com or by calling 0800 783 0033.
