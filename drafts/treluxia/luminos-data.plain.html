The LUMINOS clinical programme

TRELUXIA™ (trelimovab) demonstrated significant exacerbation reduction, lung function improvement, and oral corticosteroid sparing across the full spectrum of severe asthma in two Phase III trials.¹ ²

Broad efficacy across severe asthma phenotypes

The LUMINOS programme evaluated trelimovab in two distinct severe asthma populations: LUMINOS-1 assessed exacerbation reduction and lung function in 1,061 adults and adolescents with uncontrolled severe asthma across all inflammatory phenotypes. LUMINOS-OCS assessed oral corticosteroid sparing in 407 adults with OCS-dependent severe asthma, demonstrating the potential to reduce the burden of chronic systemic corticosteroid use.¹ ²

LUMINOS-1: Exacerbation reduction

LUMINOS-1: 56% reduction in asthma exacerbations across all phenotypes

In LUMINOS-1, a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 1,061 adults and adolescents with severe asthma on medium- or high-dose ICS plus additional controllers, TRELUXIA reduced the annualised asthma exacerbation rate by 56% versus placebo across the overall population (rate ratio 0.44; 95% CI: 0.37–0.53; P<0.001). The benefit was observed irrespective of baseline blood eosinophil count: 62% reduction in patients with eosinophils ≥300 cells/μL (RR 0.38; P<0.001) and 41% reduction in patients with eosinophils <300 cells/μL (RR 0.59; P<0.001).¹

TRELUXIA significantly improved pre-bronchodilator FEV₁ by 0.25 L at Week 52 versus placebo (P<0.001). Patients also reported clinically meaningful improvements in asthma symptom control (ACQ-6 difference: −0.48; P<0.001). Exacerbations requiring hospitalisation or emergency room visits were reduced by 77% (rate ratio 0.23; P<0.001).¹

LUMINOS-OCS: Oral corticosteroid sparing

LUMINOS-OCS: Enabling steroid freedom for OCS-dependent patients

LUMINOS-OCS was a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 407 adults with severe asthma dependent on maintenance oral corticosteroids (≥5 mg prednisolone equivalent daily) receiving high-dose ICS plus additional controllers.²

TRELUXIA enabled a median 75% reduction in OCS dose at Week 48, compared with 25% for placebo (odds ratio 2.58; P<0.001). Over one-third of TRELUXIA-treated patients (37.9%) achieved complete OCS elimination, compared with 10.8% of placebo patients (OR 5.07; P<0.001). Critically, OCS reduction was achieved while maintaining asthma control: the AAER during the OCS reduction phase was 61% lower with TRELUXIA than placebo (RR 0.39; P<0.001), and ACQ-6 scores improved significantly (difference: −0.73; P<0.001).²

Prescribing Information

TRELUXIA (trelimovab) 210 mg/1.91 mL solution for injection in pre-filled syringe and pre-filled pen. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Add-on maintenance treatment for adults and adolescents aged ≥12 years with severe asthma that is inadequately controlled despite high-dose inhaled corticosteroids plus at least one additional controller, irrespective of inflammatory phenotype. Dosage and administration: 210 mg administered by subcutaneous injection once every 4 weeks. Administer into the thigh, abdomen (excluding 5 cm around the navel), or upper arm (if administered by a caregiver). Rotate injection sites. Do not inject into areas that are tender, bruised, erythematous, or indurated. The first 3 doses should be administered under the supervision of a healthcare professional. Thereafter, a patient or caregiver may administer TRELUXIA after training in subcutaneous injection technique. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to trelimovab or any excipient. Warnings and precautions: Do not use to treat acute asthma symptoms or acute exacerbations. Do not abruptly discontinue systemic or inhaled corticosteroids upon initiation of TRELUXIA. Reduce corticosteroids gradually under medical supervision. Hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylaxis have been reported. If a serious hypersensitivity reaction occurs, discontinue TRELUXIA and initiate appropriate therapy. Treat pre-existing helminth infections before initiating TRELUXIA. Interactions: No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted. Population pharmacokinetic analyses indicated that commonly used asthma medications (ICS, LABA, LTRA, LAMA, OCS, theophylline) did not affect the pharmacokinetics of trelimovab. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): nasopharyngitis. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): upper respiratory tract infection, headache, injection site reactions, arthralgia. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): anaphylaxis, pharyngitis. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 1 pre-filled syringe (210 mg): £1,267.00; 1 pre-filled pen (210 mg): £1,267.00. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0643. Full prescribing information available from: AstraZeneca UK Ltd, 2 Pancras Square, London N1C 4AG.
