Safety profile of ELUVION

The safety of ELUVION was evaluated in 828 patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma across the HORIZON programme, with a median exposure of 14.2 months.1 2

A well-characterised safety profile consistent with HIF-2α inhibition

Serious adverse events were less frequent with ELUVION than with comparator (22.4% vs 28.1%), driven by fewer disease-related complications. Treatment discontinuation due to adverse events was 8.2%, and the most commonly reported adverse events were hypertension (18.4%), fatigue (14.1%), and anaemia (11.2%).1 2

Hypertension management

Hypertension was the most frequently reported adverse event (18.4%), consistent with the anti-angiogenic mechanism. Grade 3 hypertension occurred in 8.1% of patients. Blood pressure should be optimised prior to initiating ELUVION and monitored regularly during treatment. Standard antihypertensive therapy should be used as needed.1

Hepatic monitoring

Hepatotoxicity was reported in 4.8% of ELUVION-treated patients versus 1.2% with comparator. Grade 3–4 ALT/AST elevations occurred in 2.1%. Monitor liver function tests at baseline, every 3 weeks during the first 6 months, and periodically thereafter. Withhold ELUVION for Grade 3 elevations and permanently discontinue for Grade 4.1

Proteinuria screening

Proteinuria was reported in 7.4% of ELUVION-treated patients. Monitor urine protein at baseline and periodically during treatment. Withhold ELUVION for urine protein ≥3 g/24 hours and permanently discontinue for nephrotic syndrome.1

Special populations

Pregnancy: ELUVION may cause foetal harm based on its mechanism of action and findings in animal reproductive toxicology studies. Advise females of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment and for 6 months after the last dose. Do not use during pregnancy unless the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the foetus.1

Elderly patients: No dose adjustment is required in patients aged ≥65 years. In HORIZON-1, 38% of patients were aged ≥65 years; efficacy and safety were consistent with the overall population. Limited data are available in patients aged ≥75 years.1

Renal impairment: No dose adjustment is required for mild-to-moderate renal impairment (CrCl 30–89 mL/min). ELUVION has not been studied in patients with severe renal impairment (CrCl <30 mL/min) or end-stage renal disease.1

Hepatic impairment: No dose adjustment for mild hepatic impairment (total bilirubin ≤ULN and AST >ULN, or total bilirubin >1.0–1.5× ULN). Not recommended in moderate or severe hepatic impairment.1

Prescribing Information

ELUVION (eluvionimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. ELUVION 200 mg concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 200 mg eluvionimab in 10 mL (20 mg/mL). Indication: Treatment of adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) with clear cell histology, as monotherapy or in combination with standard of care, who have not received prior systemic therapy for advanced disease. Posology: 200 mg administered as an intravenous infusion over 60 minutes every 3 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.

Adverse Event Reporting

Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store. Adverse events should also be reported to AstraZeneca by visiting contactazmedical.astrazeneca.com or by calling 0800 783 0033.
