Targeting HIF-2α: the upstream driver of tumour angiogenesis in clear cell RCC

ELUVION selectively inhibits HIF-2α, preventing the transcription of VEGF, PDGF, and other pro-angiogenic factors that drive tumour vascularisation and growth in clear cell renal cell carcinoma.1

VHL loss and HIF-2α accumulation: the hallmark of clear cell RCC

Approximately 90% of clear cell renal cell carcinomas harbour biallelic inactivation of the Von Hippel-Lindau (VHL) tumour suppressor gene. Under normal oxygen conditions, VHL targets HIF-2α for proteasomal degradation. When VHL function is lost, HIF-2α accumulates constitutively in the cell nucleus regardless of oxygen levels, driving persistent transcription of pro-tumourigenic genes.1 3

HIF-2α is recognised as the critical oncogenic driver in VHL-deficient clear cell RCC, distinguishing it from HIF-1α which plays a more complex, sometimes tumour-suppressive role in this context.3

From one transcription factor to many tumour-promoting pathways

Constitutive HIF-2α activation drives multiple hallmarks of cancer progression simultaneously. It upregulates VEGF-A and PDGF-β to promote tumour angiogenesis and vascular remodelling, activates cyclin D1 and TGF-α to drive tumour cell proliferation, and modulates PD-L1 expression to facilitate immune evasion.1 3

Current anti-angiogenic therapies target VEGF or its receptors downstream of HIF-2α, leaving other HIF-2α–dependent pathways (proliferation, immune evasion, metabolic reprogramming) intact. This downstream approach may contribute to the development of resistance observed with existing TKI therapies.3

ELUVION: silencing HIF-2α at the transcriptional level

Eluvionimab is a fully human monoclonal antibody that binds to HIF-2α with high affinity and specificity, preventing its dimerisation with HIF-1β (ARNT) and blocking its transcriptional activity. By acting at the level of the master transcription factor rather than individual downstream effectors, ELUVION simultaneously suppresses VEGF-driven angiogenesis, tumour proliferation, and immune evasion.1

This comprehensive pathway blockade is reflected in the clinical data: an objective response rate of 47%, including 12% complete responses, and a median PFS of 14.2 months in the HORIZON-1 trial — demonstrating that upstream HIF-2α inhibition translates to meaningful clinical benefit.1 4

Why HIF-2α matters in clear cell RCC

HIF-2α sits at the apex of the oncogenic cascade in VHL-deficient clear cell RCC. As the master transcription factor driving angiogenesis, proliferation, and immune evasion simultaneously, it represents a uniquely strategic therapeutic target — addressing the root cause of tumour progression rather than individual downstream consequences.1 3

Prescribing Information

ELUVION (eluvionimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. ELUVION 200 mg concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 200 mg eluvionimab in 10 mL (20 mg/mL). Indication: Treatment of adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) with clear cell histology, as monotherapy or in combination with standard of care, who have not received prior systemic therapy for advanced disease. Posology: 200 mg administered as an intravenous infusion over 60 minutes every 3 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.
