Dosing and administration

VELOX® (veloxaban) offers a simplified dosing regimen with no routine dose adjustments required for age, body weight, or renal function (CrCl ≥30 mL/min).³

One dose. Once daily. No routine adjustments.

VELOX 40 mg once daily — taken with or without food. Unlike current DOACs, no dose reduction is required for moderate renal impairment, advanced age, or low body weight, reducing prescribing complexity and the risk of inappropriate dose modifications.³

Recommended dose

The recommended dose of VELOX is 40 mg (one tablet) taken orally once daily, with or without food. Tablets should be swallowed whole and must not be crushed, split, or chewed. If a dose is missed, it should be taken as soon as remembered on the same day. Do not take a double dose to make up for a missed dose.³

No dose adjustment is required for: age (including patients ≥75 and ≥80 years); body weight (including <60 kg and >120 kg); moderate renal impairment (CrCl 30–49 mL/min); mild to moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh A or B). VELOX is contraindicated in patients with CrCl <15 mL/min and is not recommended in CrCl 15–29 mL/min due to lack of clinical data.³

Monitoring and practical considerations

Routine coagulation monitoring is not required with VELOX. Assess renal function (CrCl) before initiation and at least annually during treatment, or more frequently if clinically indicated (acute illness, dehydration, intercurrent renal disease). Assess hepatic function before initiation; routine hepatic monitoring during treatment is not required.³

Switching from warfarin: Discontinue warfarin and start VELOX when INR falls below 2.0. Switching from rivaroxaban, apixaban, edoxaban, or dabigatran: Start VELOX at the time of the next scheduled dose of the previous anticoagulant. Perioperative management: Discontinue VELOX at least 24 hours before elective surgery with standard bleeding risk, or 48 hours for high-bleeding-risk procedures. Resume when adequate haemostasis is established, typically within 24 hours post-procedure.³

Key drug interactions

Avoid concomitant use with strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, ritonavir, lopinavir) — these increase veloxaban exposure approximately 2.1-fold. Avoid strong CYP3A4/P-gp inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin, St John’s wort) as these may reduce efficacy. No dose adjustment is required with moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors (diltiazem, verapamil, erythromycin) or with PPIs, H&sub2;-receptor antagonists, or antacids.³

Prescribing Information

VELOX (veloxaban) 40 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Prevention of stroke and systemic embolism in adult patients with non-valvular atrial fibrillation with one or more risk factors for stroke (prior stroke/TIA/SE, age ≥75 years, hypertension, diabetes mellitus, heart failure NYHA ≥II). Dosage and administration: 40 mg once daily, with or without food. Swallow whole; do not crush, split, or chew. No dose adjustment required for age, body weight, or renal impairment (CrCl ≥30 mL/min). Contraindicated in CrCl <15 mL/min. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to veloxaban or excipients; active clinically significant bleeding; hepatic disease associated with coagulopathy (Child-Pugh C); concomitant treatment with strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors; lesion or condition at significant risk of major bleeding; prosthetic heart valve requiring anticoagulant treatment. Warnings and precautions: Assess renal function before initiation and when clinically indicated during treatment. Discontinue if severe hepatic impairment develops. If switching from a vitamin K antagonist, discontinue VKA and start VELOX when INR <2.0. Reversal: No specific antidote; andexanet alfa is not expected to reverse veloxaban. Manage major bleeding with standard supportive measures and consider prothrombin complex concentrate. Interactions: Avoid strong dual CYP3A4/P-gp inhibitors (ketoconazole, itraconazole, ritonavir). Strong CYP3A4/P-gp inducers (rifampicin, carbamazepine, phenytoin) may reduce efficacy; avoid concomitant use. Side effects: Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): epistaxis, contusion, gingival bleeding, haematuria. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): intracranial haemorrhage, gastrointestinal haemorrhage, ALT increase. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 28 tablets (40 mg): £58.80; 56 tablets (40 mg): £117.60. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0721.
