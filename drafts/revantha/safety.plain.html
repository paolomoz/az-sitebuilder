Safety profile of REVANTHA

The safety of REVANTHA was evaluated in 1,221 patients with metastatic NSCLC across the ASCEND clinical programme, with a median treatment duration of 8.4 months.1 2

Well-characterised immune-related safety profile

The overall safety profile of REVANTHA was consistent with the known safety profile of immune checkpoint inhibitors. The rate of serious adverse events was numerically lower in the REVANTHA group compared with comparator (31.2% vs 34.8%).1 2

Immune-mediated adverse events

Thyroid disorders: The most common immune-mediated AEs were thyroid disorders (14.8%), predominantly hypothyroidism (10.4%). Most were Grade 1–2 and managed with hormone replacement.1

Pneumonitis: Immune-related pneumonitis occurred in 5.2% of patients (Grade 3–4: 1.8%). Treatment should be withheld for Grade 2 and permanently discontinued for Grade 3–4.1

Hepatitis: Immune-related hepatitis occurred in 3.8% of patients. Monitor liver function before and periodically during treatment.1

Colitis: Immune-related colitis occurred in 2.6% of patients. Withhold for Grade 2–3 and permanently discontinue for Grade 4.1

Infusion reactions and special populations

Infusion-related reactions: Occurred in 6.3% of patients (Grade 3–4: 0.8%). Pre-medication is not routinely required. If Grade 1–2 reactions occur, reduce the infusion rate. Permanently discontinue for Grade 3–4.1

Pregnancy and breastfeeding: REVANTHA is contraindicated during pregnancy. Women of childbearing potential must use effective contraception during treatment and for 5 months after the last dose. Breastfeeding is not recommended.1

Elderly patients: No dose adjustment is required in patients aged ≥65 years. No clinically meaningful differences in safety or efficacy were observed.1

Autoimmune disease: Use with caution in patients with a history of autoimmune disease; risk of immune-mediated exacerbation.1

Prescribing Information

REVANTHA (revantholimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

REVANTHA 20 mg/mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 1200 mg revantholimab in 60 mL.

Indication: First-line treatment of adult patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥1%) with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations, in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy. Monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC after prior platinum-based chemotherapy, whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%).

Posology: 1200 mg intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. First infusion over 60 minutes; subsequent infusions may be reduced to 30 minutes if tolerated.

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Pregnancy.
