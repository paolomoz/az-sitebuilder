NOVAPRIL (novaprylimab)

First-in-class dual BAFF/APRIL inhibitor for the treatment of active systemic lupus erythematosus in adult patients with an inadequate response to standard therapy.1

Systemic lupus erythematosus: a complex, unpredictable challenge

SLE affects approximately 50,000 people in the UK, with a disproportionate impact on women of childbearing age and individuals of Black, Asian, and Hispanic heritage.1 2 Despite available therapies, many patients continue to experience disease flares, progressive organ damage, and the cumulative burden of long-term corticosteroid use.3

Proven efficacy in SLE and lupus nephritis

NOVAPRIL demonstrated significant improvements in SRI-4 response and complete renal response across the LUMINOS clinical programme.1 2

Well-characterised safety profile

Adverse events were generally consistent with the known safety profile of B-cell modulating therapies. The most common adverse event was upper respiratory tract infection.1

Review safety profile

Simple monthly dosing

Following a loading period, NOVAPRIL is administered as a 200 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks via a pre-filled pen.1

See dosing guide

Understanding dual BAFF/APRIL inhibition

NOVAPRIL is the first therapy to simultaneously block both BAFF and APRIL signalling pathways, addressing the compensatory upregulation of APRIL observed with single-pathway inhibitors.1

Unlike BAFF-only blockade, dual pathway inhibition provides more comprehensive modulation of pathogenic B-cell survival and autoantibody production.1

Prescribing Information

NOVAPRIL (novaprylimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

NOVAPRIL 200 mg solution for injection in pre-filled pen. Each pre-filled pen contains 200 mg novaprylimab in 1 mL solution.

Indication: Treatment of adult patients with active systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) who have an inadequate response to standard therapy. Treatment of adult patients with active lupus nephritis (LN), Class III, IV, or V (± III or IV), as an add-on to standard therapy.

Posology: 200 mg subcutaneous injection at Weeks 0, 2, and 4 (loading), then 200 mg every 4 weeks (maintenance).

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Active, severe infection. Pregnancy.
