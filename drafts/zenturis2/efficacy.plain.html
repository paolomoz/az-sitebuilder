Clinical evidence for Zenturis

The ZENITH clinical programme evaluated zenturigliptin across three pivotal Phase III trials in over 11,300 adults with type 2 diabetes.1–3

The ZENITH clinical programme

Zenturigliptin was evaluated in three randomised Phase III trials: ZENITH-1 assessed glycaemic control and weight reduction versus placebo; ZENITH-2 demonstrated non-inferiority to injectable semaglutide 1 mg; and ZENITH-3 established cardiovascular benefit in a dedicated outcomes trial.1–3

ZENITH-1: Glycaemic control

Superior HbA1c reduction versus placebo

In ZENITH-1, a 52-week randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 1,246 adults with T2DM inadequately controlled on metformin, zenturigliptin 14 mg demonstrated significantly greater reductions in HbA1c from baseline versus placebo at Week 26.1

ZENITH-2: Non-inferiority vs semaglutide

Non-inferior to injectable semaglutide 1 mg

In ZENITH-2, a 52-week randomised, open-label, active-controlled trial in 1,834 adults with T2DM inadequately controlled on metformin ± sulfonylurea, zenturigliptin 14 mg demonstrated non-inferior HbA1c reduction compared with injectable semaglutide 1 mg.2

Zenturigliptin achieved non-inferior glycaemic control to injectable semaglutide 1 mg, with significantly higher patient satisfaction and preference for the oral route of administration.2

Cardiovascular risk reduction demonstrated in ZENITH-3

In the dedicated cardiovascular outcomes trial (ZENITH-3), zenturigliptin demonstrated an 18% relative risk reduction in the composite of cardiovascular death, non-fatal myocardial infarction, and non-fatal stroke versus placebo (HR 0.82; 95% CI: 0.70–0.96; P=0.013) in 8,246 adults with T2DM and established cardiovascular disease or multiple cardiovascular risk factors over a median follow-up of 3.2 years.3

Cardiovascular death was reduced by 22% (HR 0.78; 95% CI: 0.62–0.98; P=0.031).3

Prescribing Information

ZENTURIS (zenturigliptin) 14 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

Indication: Treatment of adults with insufficiently controlled type 2 diabetes mellitus as an adjunct to diet and exercise.

Dosage and administration: 14 mg once weekly, taken in the morning on an empty stomach with a sip of plain water. The tablet must be swallowed whole and not crushed, chewed, or split. No food, drink, or other oral medicinal products should be taken for at least 30 minutes after administration.

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to zenturigliptin or any excipient. Personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2.

Warnings and precautions: Not recommended in patients with severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min/1.73m²). Monitor liver function tests prior to initiation and periodically thereafter. Not a substitute for insulin. Pancreatitis: discontinue if suspected.

Pregnancy and lactation: Not recommended.

Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): nausea, diarrhoea. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): vomiting, decreased appetite, constipation, abdominal pain, ALT increase, headache.

Legal category: POM.

Pack and price: 4 tablets (28-day supply): £185.64.
