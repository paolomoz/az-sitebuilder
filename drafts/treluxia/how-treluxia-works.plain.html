Upstream control at the source of airway inflammation

TRELUXIA™ (trelimovab) blocks TSLP, an epithelial cytokine that initiates multiple inflammatory cascades in asthma — providing broad anti-inflammatory control beyond single-pathway biologics.³ &sup5;

TSLP: the upstream driver of severe asthma inflammation

Thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP) is produced by airway epithelial cells in response to environmental triggers including allergens, viral infections, pollutants, and mechanical injury.&sup5;

Once released, TSLP activates dendritic cells and innate lymphoid cells (ILC2s), initiating a cascade of downstream inflammatory events: Th2 cell differentiation and IL-4/IL-5/IL-13 production, eosinophil recruitment and activation, IgE class switching and mast cell sensitisation, and structural cell activation driving airway remodelling.&sup4; &sup5;

Crucially, TSLP also drives non-type 2 inflammatory pathways including mast cell cytokine release and neutrophil-mediated inflammation, making it relevant across the full phenotypic spectrum of severe asthma.&sup5;

How TRELUXIA blocks TSLP signalling

Trelimovab is a human IgG2λ monoclonal antibody that binds specifically to TSLP, preventing its interaction with the heterodimeric TSLP receptor complex (TSLPR/IL-7Rα) on the surface of immune cells.³

By neutralising TSLP at the top of the inflammatory cascade, trelimovab inhibits the activation of downstream type 2 and non-type 2 pathways simultaneously — reducing eosinophilic inflammation, Th2 cytokine production, IgE synthesis, mast cell activation, and non-eosinophilic inflammatory mechanisms.³ &sup5;

This broad upstream mechanism distinguishes TRELUXIA from biologics that target single downstream mediators (IL-5, IL-4/IL-13, IgE), enabling efficacy across all severe asthma phenotypes including those with low eosinophil counts and mixed inflammatory signatures.³

TRELUXIA in the severe asthma treatment pathway

Current guidelines recommend biologic therapy for patients with severe asthma that remains uncontrolled despite high-dose ICS plus additional controllers.&sup4; Existing biologic options — anti-IL-5/5R, anti-IL-4/13, and anti-IgE — primarily target type 2 inflammatory pathways and require biomarker-based patient selection.&sup4;

TRELUXIA’s upstream TSLP blockade offers a complementary approach: it is indicated irrespective of inflammatory phenotype, providing a treatment option for patients across the entire severe asthma spectrum — including those who may not be eligible for or who have responded inadequately to existing biologics targeting single downstream pathways.³ &sup4;

Prescribing Information

TRELUXIA (trelimovab) 210 mg/1.91 mL solution for injection in pre-filled syringe and pre-filled pen. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Add-on maintenance treatment for adults and adolescents aged ≥12 years with severe asthma that is inadequately controlled despite high-dose inhaled corticosteroids plus at least one additional controller, irrespective of inflammatory phenotype. Dosage and administration: 210 mg administered by subcutaneous injection once every 4 weeks. Administer into the thigh, abdomen (excluding 5 cm around the navel), or upper arm (if administered by a caregiver). Rotate injection sites. Do not inject into areas that are tender, bruised, erythematous, or indurated. The first 3 doses should be administered under the supervision of a healthcare professional. Thereafter, a patient or caregiver may administer TRELUXIA after training in subcutaneous injection technique. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to trelimovab or any excipient. Warnings and precautions: Do not use to treat acute asthma symptoms or acute exacerbations. Do not abruptly discontinue systemic or inhaled corticosteroids upon initiation of TRELUXIA. Reduce corticosteroids gradually under medical supervision. Hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylaxis have been reported. If a serious hypersensitivity reaction occurs, discontinue TRELUXIA and initiate appropriate therapy. Treat pre-existing helminth infections before initiating TRELUXIA. Interactions: No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted. Population pharmacokinetic analyses indicated that commonly used asthma medications (ICS, LABA, LTRA, LAMA, OCS, theophylline) did not affect the pharmacokinetics of trelimovab. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): nasopharyngitis. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): upper respiratory tract infection, headache, injection site reactions, arthralgia. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): anaphylaxis, pharyngitis. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 1 pre-filled syringe (210 mg): £1,267.00; 1 pre-filled pen (210 mg): £1,267.00.
