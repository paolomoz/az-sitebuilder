Simple, convenient dosing

NOVAPRIL is administered as a 200 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks following a short loading period, using a pre-filled pen designed for self-administration.1

Dosing regimen

NOVAPRIL offers a straightforward dosing schedule with no requirement for dose titration based on body weight, renal function, or hepatic function.1

Loading and maintenance dosing

Loading dose: 200 mg subcutaneous injection at Week 0, Week 2, and Week 4.1

Maintenance dose: 200 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks thereafter.1

No dose adjustment is required based on body weight, age, sex, mild-to-moderate renal impairment, or mild hepatic impairment.1

NOVAPRIL should be administered on the same day each month where possible. If a dose is missed, it should be administered as soon as possible, then dosing should resume on the regular schedule.1

Pre-filled pen for self-administration

NOVAPRIL is supplied as a single-use, pre-filled pen containing 200 mg/1 mL solution for injection.1

After appropriate training by a healthcare professional, patients or their caregivers may self-administer NOVAPRIL. Injection sites include the abdomen, thigh, or upper arm. Rotate injection sites with each dose.1

Allow the pre-filled pen to reach room temperature (approximately 30 minutes) before injection. Do not shake. Inspect visually before use — the solution should be clear to slightly opalescent, colourless to pale yellow.1

Before starting NOVAPRIL

Before initiating treatment, healthcare professionals should:1

  • Assess the patient's current disease activity using validated measures (e.g. SLEDAI-2K)
  • Screen for active infections, including tuberculosis, hepatitis B, and hepatitis C
  • Measure baseline immunoglobulin levels (IgG, IgA, IgM)
  • Review the patient's vaccination status and administer any required non-live vaccines at least 4 weeks before starting treatment
  • Ensure women of childbearing potential are using effective contraception
Prescribing Information

NOVAPRIL (novaprylimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

NOVAPRIL 200 mg solution for injection in pre-filled pen. Each pre-filled pen contains 200 mg novaprylimab in 1 mL solution.

Indication: Treatment of adult patients with active systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) who have an inadequate response to standard therapy. Treatment of adult patients with active lupus nephritis (LN), Class III, IV, or V (± III or IV), as an add-on to standard therapy.

Posology: 200 mg subcutaneous injection at Weeks 0, 2, and 4 (loading), then 200 mg every 4 weeks (maintenance).

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Active, severe infection. Pregnancy.

Adverse Event Reporting

Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store.

Adverse events should also be reported to AstraZeneca by visiting contactazmedical.astrazeneca.com or by calling 0800 783 0033.
