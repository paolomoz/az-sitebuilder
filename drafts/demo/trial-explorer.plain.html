FLAURA — Osimertinib vs Standard of Care in NSCLC

The FLAURA trial demonstrated the efficacy of osimertinib (Tagrisso) as first-line treatment for patients with EGFR mutation-positive advanced non-small cell lung cancer. This landmark Phase III study enrolled 556 patients across 29 countries.

DAPA-HF — Dapagliflozin in Heart Failure

The DAPA-HF trial evaluated the effect of dapagliflozin (Farxiga/Forxiga) on cardiovascular death and worsening heart failure in patients with heart failure and reduced ejection fraction, with or without type 2 diabetes.
