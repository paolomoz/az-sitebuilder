Selective JAK1 inhibition

XEVAT preferentially targets JAK1, blocking the signalling of key pro-inflammatory cytokines while minimising effects on JAK2, JAK3, and TYK2.1

The JAK-STAT signalling pathway

The Janus kinase (JAK)-Signal Transducer and Activator of Transcription (STAT) pathway is a critical intracellular signalling cascade that mediates the biological effects of numerous cytokines, interferons, and growth factors involved in immune regulation, haematopoiesis, and inflammation.1 2

JAK1 — a central node in RA pathogenesis

JAK1 pairs with other JAK family members to transduce signals from IL-6, IL-7, IL-15, IFN-gamma, and other cytokines that drive chronic synovial inflammation and joint destruction. JAK1 inhibition attenuates these pathways, reducing inflammatory cell activation and joint damage.1 2

The selectivity advantage

XEVAT demonstrates approximately 30-fold selectivity for JAK1 over JAK2. JAK2 mediates erythropoietin and thrombopoietin signalling, so minimising JAK2 inhibition reduces the risk of anaemia and thrombocytopenia. JAK3 inhibition is similarly minimised, preserving aspects of adaptive immunity mediated by JAK3-dependent cytokines (IL-2, IL-4, IL-21).1

Prescribing Information

XEVAT (xevatirel) 100 mg and 200 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

Indication: Treatment of moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis in adults with inadequate response or intolerance to one or more DMARDs; treatment of active psoriatic arthritis in adults with inadequate response or intolerance to a prior DMARD therapy.

Dosage: 200 mg once daily; reduce to 100 mg once daily for moderate renal impairment or with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors.

Contraindications: Severe hepatic impairment; severe renal impairment (eGFR <30 mL/min); pregnancy; active serious infection.

Warnings and precautions: Serious infections, herpes zoster, haematological abnormalities, lipid elevations — see full SmPC.

Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): upper respiratory tract infection. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): nausea, headache, urinary tract infection, elevated CPK, herpes zoster, neutropenia.

Legal category: POM. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd, 2 Pancras Square, London N1C 4AG.
