## 2. Mechanism of Action

Telvisoran is a fully human IgG4 monoclonal antibody that binds to the programmed death-1 (PD-1) receptor on T cells, blocking its interaction with PD-L1 and PD-L2 ligands expressed on tumour cells and the tumour microenvironment.

In NSCLC, tumour cells upregulate PD-L1 expression as an immune evasion mechanism, effectively suppressing the anti-tumour T-cell response. By blocking the PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint, telvisoran:

- Restores the ability of cytotoxic T cells to recognise and eliminate tumour cells
- Reactivates exhausted tumour-infiltrating lymphocytes within the tumour microenvironment
- Enhances the anti-tumour immune response through reinvigorated T-cell proliferation and cytokine production
- Promotes durable immune memory, contributing to sustained clinical responses

Telvisoran has been engineered with a stabilised IgG4 hinge region to minimise Fc-mediated effector function, reducing the risk of antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity against PD-1-expressing T cells.

## 3. Clinical Programme: TRAJECTORY

### TRAJECTORY-1 (Phase III, pivotal — First-line monotherapy vs chemotherapy)

- **Design:** Randomised, open-label, Phase III, multicentre, international
- **Population:** 637 adults with previously untreated locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC, PD-L1 TPS &ge;50%, no EGFR or ALK genomic aberrations, ECOG PS 0&ndash;1
- **Primary endpoint:** Overall survival (OS)
- **Key secondary endpoints:** Progression-free survival (PFS), objective response rate (ORR), duration of response (DOR)
- **Key results:**
  - Median OS: **26.3 months vs 14.2 months** (HR 0.63; 95% CI: 0.51&ndash;0.78; P&lt;0.001)
  - Median PFS: **10.4 months vs 6.1 months** (HR 0.58; 95% CI: 0.47&ndash;0.72; P&lt;0.001)
  - ORR: **48% vs 27%** (P&lt;0.001)
  - Median DOR: **22.8 months vs 6.3 months**
  - 2-year OS rate: **53% vs 29%**
- **Publication:** Okamoto et al. *N Engl J Med* 2025; 392(14): 1324&ndash;1338.

### TRAJECTORY-LT (Phase III — 5-year long-term follow-up of TRAJECTORY-1)

- **Design:** Pre-specified 5-year extended follow-up of the TRAJECTORY-1 intent-to-treat population
- **Population:** 637 patients from TRAJECTORY-1 (318 telvisoran, 319 chemotherapy)
- **Primary objective:** Long-term overall survival and durability of response
- **Key results:**
  - 5-year OS rate: **31.4% vs 14.6%** (HR 0.61; 95% CI: 0.50&ndash;0.74)
  - Median OS (updated): **26.8 months vs 14.2 months**
  - Ongoing responses at 5 years: **18% of initial responders** (26/144 patients who responded to telvisoran remained in response at data cut-off)
  - 5-year PFS rate: **16.8% vs 3.1%**
  - No new safety signals identified with extended follow-up
- **Publication:** Okamoto et al. *J Clin Oncol* 2026; 44(3): 412&ndash;425.

## 4. Safety Profile (Pooled data, n=1,274)

### Immune-mediated adverse reactions — Important safety information

NOVATREL can cause immune-mediated adverse reactions, which may be severe or fatal. These may involve any organ system, most commonly the lungs, liver, colon, endocrine glands, kidneys, and skin. Immune-mediated adverse reactions may occur during or after treatment discontinuation. Monitor patients for signs and symptoms. Manage with treatment interruption, corticosteroids, and permanent discontinuation as per SmPC guidance.

### Key safety findings

- **Serious adverse events:** 28.4% vs 34.6% chemotherapy
- **Treatment discontinuation due to AEs:** 9.8% vs 13.4%
- **Treatment-related deaths:** 1.3% (8 patients) — immune-mediated pneumonitis (3), hepatitis (2), myocarditis (1), encephalitis (1), pneumonia (1)
- **Immune-mediated AEs requiring systemic corticosteroids:** 18.2%
- **Immune-mediated AEs leading to permanent discontinuation:** 7.1%
- **Infusion-related reactions:** 3.2% (Grade 3&ndash;4: 0.6%)

### Special populations

- **Pregnancy:** NOVATREL may cause foetal harm based on its mechanism of action. Animal studies demonstrated increased foetal loss and neonatal death. Advise women of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment and for 4 months after the last dose.
- **Breastfeeding:** It is not known whether telvisoran is excreted in human milk. Due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in breastfed infants, advise women not to breastfeed during treatment and for 4 months after the last dose.
- **Elderly (&ge;65 years):** No dose adjustment required. Clinical trial data suggest similar efficacy and safety in patients &ge;65 years compared with the overall population. Limited data in patients &ge;75 years.
- **Hepatic impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild hepatic impairment (bilirubin &le;ULN with AST &gt;ULN, or bilirubin &gt;1.0&ndash;1.5&times; ULN). Not studied in moderate or severe hepatic impairment.
- **Renal impairment:** No dose adjustment required for mild or moderate renal impairment (CrCl 30&ndash;89 mL/min). Limited data in severe renal impairment.

Both schedules deliver equivalent drug exposure. The choice of schedule should be based on clinical judgement and patient preference.&sup3;

**Heading:** Administration and preparation

#### Section 2 — The PD-1/PD-L1 checkpoint (two-column: image left, text right)

**Block:** columns-teaser

- **Image:** `https://novatrel-images.pages.dev/columns-checkpoint.jpeg` (4:3, 800&times;600)
- **Image prompt:** "4:3 aspect ratio. Scientific illustration showing PD-1 receptor on a T cell engaging with PD-L1 on a tumour cell, resulting in T-cell exhaustion. **Heading:** How tumours evade immune detection

**Body text:** In normal immune function, the PD-1 receptor on T cells helps regulate the immune response by preventing excessive tissue damage. However, tumour cells exploit this mechanism by upregulating PD-L1 on their surface, binding to PD-1 on tumour-infiltrating T cells and effectively switching off the anti-tumour immune response.&sup3; &sup5;

In NSCLC tumours with high PD-L1 expression (TPS &ge;50%), this immune checkpoint is the dominant mechanism of immune evasion, making these tumours particularly responsive to PD-1 pathway blockade.&sup5;
