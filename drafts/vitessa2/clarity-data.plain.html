Clinically meaningful improvements across the CLARITY programme

VITESSA demonstrated significant reductions in exacerbations, improvements in lung function, and the ability to eliminate oral corticosteroid dependence across two pivotal Phase III trials.¹ ²

The CLARITY clinical programme

CLARITY was a comprehensive Phase III programme evaluating vitessamab in nearly 1,500 patients with severe asthma across two pivotal, randomised, placebo-controlled trials: CLARITY-1 (exacerbation reduction) and CLARITY-2 (oral corticosteroid sparing).¹ ²

CLARITY-1: Exacerbation Reduction

CLARITY-1: 68% reduction in exacerbations over 52 weeks

VITESSA reduced the annualised asthma exacerbation rate to 0.28 compared with 0.87 for placebo — a 68% reduction (rate ratio 0.32; 95% CI: 0.25–0.41; P<0.001). This included a 78% reduction in exacerbations requiring hospitalisation or emergency department visits.¹

Patients also experienced a 230 mL improvement in pre-bronchodilator FEV1 compared with placebo at Week 52, and 72% achieved a clinically meaningful improvement in asthma control (ACQ-6 responder rate).¹

CLARITY-2: OCS Sparing

CLARITY-2: 58% of patients completely eliminated oral corticosteroids

Among patients dependent on daily oral corticosteroids, 58% treated with VITESSA achieved complete OCS elimination compared with 19% on placebo (P<0.001). The median OCS dose reduction was 82% versus 50% with placebo.²

Critically, asthma control was maintained throughout OCS reduction, and patients on VITESSA continued to show FEV1 improvements (+190 mL vs −20 mL with placebo) despite corticosteroid withdrawal.²

Prescribing Information

VITESSA (vitessamab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. VITESSA 210 mg solution for injection in a pre-filled pen. Each pen contains 210 mg vitessamab in 1.4 mL. Indication: Add-on maintenance treatment in adults with severe eosinophilic asthma inadequately controlled despite high-dose ICS plus a LABA. Posology: 210 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.

Adverse Event Reporting
