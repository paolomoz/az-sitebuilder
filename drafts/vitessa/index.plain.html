VITESSA (vitessamab)

The first anti-IL-33 therapy for severe eosinophilic asthma. Targeting the alarmin that triggers it all.1

Explore the CLARITY data

68% fewer exacerbations at one year

In CLARITY-1, VITESSA reduced the annualised exacerbation rate by 68% compared with placebo in patients with severe eosinophilic asthma on high-dose ICS/LABA.1

View CLARITY efficacy data

Fewer serious adverse events than placebo

Serious adverse events occurred in 8.2% of VITESSA-treated patients compared with 12.1% in the placebo group, driven by fewer asthma-related hospitalisations.1

One injection, once a month, at home

VITESSA is a 210 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks, self-administered via a pre-filled pen after appropriate training. No loading dose required.1

Severe eosinophilic asthma: a persistent burden despite current biologics

Approximately 5–10% of asthma patients have severe disease, and many remain inadequately controlled despite available biologic therapies targeting downstream mediators.1 2 Exacerbations continue to drive hospitalisations, oral corticosteroid dependence, and progressive loss of lung function, underscoring the need for therapies that address inflammation at its source.3

Blocking inflammation at the source

IL-33 is the upstream alarmin that initiates the type 2 inflammatory cascade. By neutralising IL-33 before it activates ILC2 cells, mast cells, and eosinophils, VITESSA addresses severe eosinophilic asthma at the apex of the pathway — not just downstream.1

Prescribing Information

VITESSA (vitessamab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. VITESSA 210 mg solution for injection in a pre-filled pen. Each pen contains 210 mg vitessamab in 1.4 mL. Indication: Add-on maintenance treatment in adults with severe eosinophilic asthma inadequately controlled despite high-dose ICS plus a LABA. Posology: 210 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.

Adverse Event Reporting

Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store. Adverse events should also be reported to AstraZeneca by visiting contactazmedical.astrazeneca.com or by calling 0800 783 0033.
