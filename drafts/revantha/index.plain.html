REVANTHA (revantholimab)

First-in-class dual PD-L1/TIGIT checkpoint inhibitor for the first-line treatment of metastatic non-small cell lung cancer in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy.1

Explore efficacy data

Non-small cell lung cancer: the leading cause of cancer mortality

Lung cancer remains the leading cause of cancer-related death worldwide, with NSCLC accounting for approximately 85% of all lung cancer diagnoses.1 2 Despite advances in immunotherapy, many patients with metastatic NSCLC experience disease progression on current first-line regimens, highlighting the need for more effective immune-based strategies.3

Significant survival benefit in first-line NSCLC

REVANTHA + chemotherapy demonstrated a 44% reduction in risk of death and near doubling of median PFS compared with chemotherapy alone in the ASCEND-1 trial.1

View efficacy data

Manageable safety profile

Immune-mediated adverse events were consistent with the known profile of checkpoint inhibitors, with the majority being Grade 1–2 and manageable with established guidelines.1

Review safety profile

Convenient 3-weekly dosing

REVANTHA is administered as a 1200 mg intravenous infusion every 3 weeks — a single bispecific agent replacing the need for combination checkpoint blockade.1

See dosing guide

Dual checkpoint blockade in a single molecule

REVANTHA simultaneously blocks PD-L1 and TIGIT — two co-inhibitory pathways exploited by tumours to evade immune detection — restoring anti-tumour T-cell function more effectively than PD-L1 blockade alone.1

Prescribing Information

REVANTHA (revantholimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing.

REVANTHA 20 mg/mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 1200 mg revantholimab in 60 mL.

Indication: First-line treatment of adult patients with metastatic non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥1%) with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations, in combination with platinum-based chemotherapy. Monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC after prior platinum-based chemotherapy, whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%).

Posology: 1200 mg intravenous infusion every 3 weeks. First infusion over 60 minutes; subsequent infusions may be reduced to 30 minutes if tolerated.

Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient. Pregnancy.

Adverse Event Reporting

Adverse events should be reported. Reporting forms and information can be found at www.mhra.gov.uk/yellowcard or search for MHRA Yellow Card in the Google Play or Apple App Store.
