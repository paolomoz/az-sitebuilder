The RENOVA clinical programme

SERANOVA™ (valinectra) demonstrated significant kidney and cardiovascular protection in over 11,000 adults with chronic kidney disease and type 2 diabetes across two Phase III trials.1 2

Comprehensive cardiorenal evidence across two pivotal trials

The RENOVA programme evaluated valinectra in two distinct but complementary populations: RENOVA-CKD assessed kidney and cardiovascular outcomes in 7,352 adults with CKD and type 2 diabetes, establishing the primary indication. RENOVA-HF assessed cardiovascular outcomes in 3,814 adults with coexisting heart failure, CKD, and type 2 diabetes, extending the evidence to the highest-risk patients.1 2

RENOVA-CKD: Kidney outcomes

RENOVA-CKD: 23% reduction in the composite kidney outcome

In RENOVA-CKD, a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 7,352 adults with CKD (eGFR 25–75 mL/min/1.73m², UACR 30–5,000 mg/g) and type 2 diabetes on maximum tolerated RAAS inhibition, SERANOVA reduced the primary composite endpoint (sustained ≥57% eGFR decline, ESKD, renal death, or CV death) by 23% versus placebo (HR 0.77; 95% CI: 0.67–0.88; P<0.001).1

SERANOVA slowed the rate of kidney function decline, with a total eGFR slope of −2.1 vs −3.8 mL/min/1.73m²/year (difference: +1.7; P<0.001). Albuminuria was reduced by 36% from baseline at Month 4 (ratio 0.65; P<0.001). The cardiovascular co-primary endpoint (CV death or HHF) was also significantly reduced (HR 0.82; P=0.005).1

RENOVA-HF: Heart failure outcomes

RENOVA-HF: 18% reduction in CV death or hospitalisation for heart failure

RENOVA-HF was a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial in 3,814 adults with heart failure (NYHA II–IV, LVEF ≤40%), CKD (eGFR 25–60 mL/min/1.73m²), and type 2 diabetes receiving guideline-directed medical therapy including RAAS inhibition and SGLT2 inhibitors.2

SERANOVA reduced the primary composite of cardiovascular death or hospitalisation for heart failure by 18% (HR 0.82; 95% CI: 0.72–0.93; P=0.002). Hospitalisations for heart failure were reduced by 20% (HR 0.80; P=0.007). Patients reported clinically meaningful improvements in heart failure symptoms (KCCQ-TSS difference: +2.9 points; P<0.001). SERANOVA also slowed kidney function decline (eGFR slope: −1.8 vs −3.2 mL/min/1.73m²/year; P<0.001).2

Prescribing Information

SERANOVA (valinectra) 10 mg and 20 mg film-coated tablets. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Reduction of sustained eGFR decline, end-stage kidney disease, cardiovascular death, and hospitalisation for heart failure in adults with chronic kidney disease associated with type 2 diabetes mellitus. Dosage and administration: Initiate at 10 mg once daily. Measure serum potassium before initiation and within 4 weeks. If serum potassium ≤4.8 mmol/L, uptitrate to 20 mg once daily. If serum potassium >4.8 to 5.0 mmol/L, maintain 10 mg. If serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L, withhold and recheck. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to valinectra or any excipient. Concomitant use with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors (e.g. itraconazole, ketoconazole, ritonavir, clarithromycin). Addison’s disease. Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh C). Warnings and precautions: Monitor serum potassium before initiation, at 4 weeks, and periodically thereafter. Interrupt treatment if potassium >5.5 mmol/L. Do not initiate if serum potassium >5.0 mmol/L or eGFR <25 mL/min/1.73m². Use with caution in patients receiving potassium-sparing diuretics or potassium supplements. An initial eGFR decline may occur upon treatment initiation; this is typically reversible and not a reason to discontinue. Interactions: Contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors. Avoid grapefruit juice. Moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors: no dose adjustment required but monitor potassium more frequently. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): none. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): hyperkalaemia, hypotension. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): dizziness, pruritus, hyponatraemia. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 28 tablets (10 mg): £524.60; 28 tablets (20 mg): £524.60. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0527.
