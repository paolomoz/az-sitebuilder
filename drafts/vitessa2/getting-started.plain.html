Simple monthly self-injection

VITESSA is a 210 mg subcutaneous injection administered once every 4 weeks using a pre-filled pen. No loading dose. No weight-based dosing. No clinic visits required after training.¹

Step 1: Assess eligibility

Confirm the diagnosis of severe eosinophilic asthma and document blood eosinophil count ≥300 cells/µL. Patients should have demonstrated inadequate control despite optimised high-dose ICS/LABA therapy. Screen for and treat pre-existing helminth infections. Consider varicella zoster vaccination in eligible patients prior to initiation.¹

Step 2: Initiate and train

Prescribe VITESSA 210 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks. Provide injection training using the VITESSA Pen demonstration device. VITESSA is not indicated for the relief of acute bronchospasm or status asthmaticus. Patients should not discontinue or reduce their existing controller medications (including ICS) upon initiation unless directed by their physician.¹

Step 3: Support self-management

Patients self-administer at home after training. The VITESSA Pen delivers the full dose in approximately 10 seconds. A window on the pen confirms when the injection is complete. Rotate injection sites: abdomen, thigh, or upper arm (caregiver only). Schedule 4-weekly follow-up to monitor asthma control and eosinophil levels.¹

Designed for patient convenience

VITESSA is the first anti-IL-33 biologic available as a single fixed-dose, room-temperature-stable pre-filled pen. The simplified dosing regimen supports patient adherence and reduces the treatment burden associated with severe asthma management.¹

Storage and handling

Store refrigerated at 2–8°C in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze. Do not shake.¹

VITESSA may be removed from the refrigerator and stored at room temperature (up to 25°C) for a maximum of 7 days. Record the date of removal from the refrigerator. Discard if not used within 7 days at room temperature. Do not return to the refrigerator once warmed to room temperature.¹

Before injection, remove the pen from the refrigerator and allow it to reach room temperature for 30 minutes. Do not warm by any other means. Inspect visually: the solution should be clear to slightly opalescent, colourless to pale yellow, and free from visible particles.¹

Prescribing Information

VITESSA (vitessamab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. VITESSA 210 mg solution for injection in a pre-filled pen. Each pen contains 210 mg vitessamab in 1.4 mL. Indication: Add-on maintenance treatment in adults with severe eosinophilic asthma inadequately controlled despite high-dose ICS plus a LABA. Posology: 210 mg subcutaneous injection every 4 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.
