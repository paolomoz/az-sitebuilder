Safety profile of NOVATREL

NOVATREL™ (telvisoran) has been evaluated in 636 patients with advanced NSCLC in the TRAJECTORY clinical programme.¹ ² ³

Immune-mediated adverse reactions

NOVATREL can cause immune-mediated adverse reactions, which may be severe or fatal, including pneumonitis, hepatitis, colitis, endocrinopathies (hypothyroidism, hyperthyroidism, adrenal insufficiency, type 1 diabetes mellitus), nephritis, and severe skin reactions. These may occur at any time during or after treatment. Monitor patients for signs and symptoms of immune-mediated adverse reactions. Manage with treatment interruption, systemic corticosteroids, and permanent discontinuation as per SmPC guidance.³

A manageable safety profile consistent with anti-PD-1 immunotherapy

Serious adverse events occurred in 28.4% of NOVATREL-treated patients compared with 34.6% in the chemotherapy group. Treatment discontinuation due to adverse events was lower with NOVATREL (9.8% vs 13.4%). The safety profile was consistent with the known class effects of PD-1 checkpoint inhibitors, with immune-mediated adverse events being the principal safety consideration.¹ ³

Immune-mediated pneumonitis

Pneumonitis occurred in 5.8% of NOVATREL-treated patients (Grade 3–4: 2.1%). Withhold NOVATREL for Grade 2 pneumonitis and treat with corticosteroids. Permanently discontinue for Grade 3 or 4, or recurrent Grade 2 pneumonitis.³

Immune-mediated hepatitis

Hepatitis occurred in 1.9% of patients (Grade 3–4: 0.8%). Monitor liver function tests at baseline and periodically during treatment. Withhold for Grade 2 AST/ALT elevation; permanently discontinue for Grade 3 or 4.³

Endocrinopathies

Hypothyroidism (5.4%) and hyperthyroidism (3.6%) were the most common endocrinopathies. Monitor thyroid function at baseline and periodically during treatment. Manage with hormone replacement therapy as clinically indicated. NOVATREL may generally be continued with appropriate endocrine management.³

Special populations

Pregnancy and fertility: NOVATREL may cause foetal harm based on its mechanism of action. Advise women of reproductive potential to use effective contraception during treatment and for 4 months after the last dose. Verify pregnancy status before initiating treatment.³

Elderly patients: No dose adjustment is required in patients aged ≥65 years. Efficacy and safety were generally consistent with the overall population. Limited data in patients aged ≥75 years.³

Hepatic impairment: No dose adjustment required for mild hepatic impairment. NOVATREL has not been studied in moderate or severe hepatic impairment.³

Renal impairment: No dose adjustment required for mild or moderate renal impairment. Limited data in severe renal impairment.³

Prescribing Information

NOVATREL (telvisoran) 200 mg/10 mL concentrate for solution for infusion. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: First-line monotherapy for adults with locally advanced or metastatic NSCLC whose tumours express PD-L1 (TPS ≥50%), with no EGFR or ALK genomic tumour aberrations. Dosage: 200 mg by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes every 3 weeks, or 400 mg every 6 weeks. Treat until disease progression, unacceptable toxicity, or up to 24 months. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to telvisoran or any excipient. Warnings and precautions: Immune-mediated adverse reactions may be severe or fatal. Monitor for signs and symptoms of pneumonitis, hepatitis, colitis, endocrinopathies, nephritis, and skin reactions. Manage with dose interruption, corticosteroids, or permanent discontinuation as appropriate. Infusion-related reactions have been reported. Pregnancy and lactation: May cause foetal harm; effective contraception required during treatment and for 4 months after. Breastfeeding not recommended during treatment and for 4 months after. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): fatigue, musculoskeletal pain, decreased appetite, rash, diarrhoea, nausea. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): cough, pruritus, dyspnoea, constipation, pneumonitis, hypothyroidism. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 1 vial (200 mg/10 mL): £2,630.00.
