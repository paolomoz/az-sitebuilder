Clinically meaningful improvements across the HORIZON programme

ELUVION demonstrated significant improvements in progression-free survival, overall survival, and objective response rate across two pivotal Phase III trials in advanced renal cell carcinoma.1 2

The HORIZON clinical programme

HORIZON was a comprehensive Phase III programme evaluating eluvionimab in over 1,600 patients with advanced clear cell renal cell carcinoma across two pivotal, randomised, active-controlled trials: HORIZON-1 (first-line progression-free survival) and HORIZON-2 (second-line overall survival).1 2

HORIZON-1: First-line PFS

HORIZON-1: 42% improvement in progression-free survival

ELUVION demonstrated a median progression-free survival of 14.2 months compared with 8.4 months for sunitinib in previously untreated advanced clear cell RCC — a 42% improvement (HR 0.59; 95% CI: 0.48–0.73; P<0.001). The objective response rate was 47% versus 27% (P<0.001), with a median duration of response of 18.6 months versus 11.2 months.1

HORIZON-2: Second-line OS

HORIZON-2: 32% improvement in overall survival

In patients with advanced RCC who had progressed on prior anti-angiogenic therapy, ELUVION demonstrated a median overall survival of 28.4 months compared with 19.8 months for everolimus — a 32% improvement (HR 0.68; 95% CI: 0.56–0.82; P<0.001). Median PFS was 11.8 months versus 5.6 months (HR 0.48; 95% CI: 0.39–0.59; P<0.001).2

Prescribing Information

ELUVION (eluvionimab) Prescribing Information. Please refer to the full Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. ELUVION 200 mg concentrate for solution for infusion. Each vial contains 200 mg eluvionimab in 10 mL (20 mg/mL). Indication: Treatment of adult patients with advanced renal cell carcinoma (RCC) with clear cell histology, as monotherapy or in combination with standard of care, who have not received prior systemic therapy for advanced disease. Posology: 200 mg administered as an intravenous infusion over 60 minutes every 3 weeks. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to the active substance or any excipient.
