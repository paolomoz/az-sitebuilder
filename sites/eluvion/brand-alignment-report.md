# Eluvion Briefing — Brand Alignment Report

> Generated 2026-03-03 by comparing the original Eluvion briefing against AZ brand guidelines
> (`brand/az-brand-voice.md`, `brand/myastrazeneca-analysis.md`, `CLAUDE.md`)

---

## Critical Issues

### 1. Wrong audience register — consumer-facing, not HCP

The entire briefing reads like a consumer pharmaceutical website or investor PR piece. AZ HCP sites speak to doctors, nurses, and pharmacists as clinical peers. The brand voice guide is explicit: "Not consumer-facing — never dumbs down clinical terminology" and "Not self-congratulatory — centres the patient and HCP, not the company."

Offending examples:
- "A Beacon of Hope in the Fight Against [Target Disease]" — dramatic, emotive
- "The Eluvion Odyssey — A Journey of Innovation and Hope" — literary/promotional
- "Launching Hope, Transforming Futures" — tagline for a consumer campaign
- "chronicling the remarkable journey" — self-congratulatory narrative
- "Empowering Patients. Transforming Futures." — slogan, not a CTA
- "A Glimmer of Hope" — emotional appeal inappropriate for HCPs

AZ would write: "Discover the [clinical benefit] of ELUVION® (generic name)" or "[Target Disease]: a growing concern"

### 2. Missing all regulatory elements

No AZ HCP page can exist without these. The briefing contains zero:
- No ® or ®▼ trademark on "Eluvion" (should be **ELUVION®** or **ELUVION®▼**)
- No generic name in parentheses after brand name
- No SmPC indication language ("indicated for the treatment of...")
- No approval code (GB-XXXXX | DOP: Month Year)
- No AE reporting boilerplate (mandatory verbatim text on every page)
- No Prescribing Information section
- No superscript reference numbers on clinical claims
- No statistical context (HR, CI, p-values, trial names) on any efficacy claims
- No abbreviations section

### 3. No actual clinical data

The briefing talks about clinical trials but contains zero data. AZ product pages lead with evidence: "56% reduction in exacerbations (RR=0.44, 95% CI: 0.37-0.53; P<0.001)". This briefing has vague language like "rigorous data analysis" and "key efficacy data" without any numbers. An HCP will find nothing actionable here.

### 4. Placeholder disease/mechanism

"[Target Disease]" and "[specific pathway/mechanism]" appear throughout. Without a real disease context, the copy can't be clinically precise, the imagery can't be appropriately themed, and the page structure can't be optimised for the content type.

## Structural Issues

### 5. Wrong page architecture

The 6-page structure doesn't match AZ product page conventions. AZ products use:

| AZ Standard | Eluvion Briefing | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| Home (product overview + indication) | Page 1 ("Odyssey") | Missing indication language, no data |
| Efficacy & clinical trials | Page 3 (partial) | Buries data behind narrative |
| Safety profile | Missing entirely | Required page |
| Dosing & administration | Missing entirely | Required page |
| Mechanism of action | Page 2 (partial) | Too narrative, no real MOA detail |
| Resources | Missing | Standard AZ page |
| Patient support (Page 4) | N/A | Patient-facing, not HCP-facing |
| Future innovations (Page 5) | N/A | Corporate PR, not clinical |
| Contact with social media (Page 6) | N/A | Consumer model |

**Recommended page structure:**
1. Home — product overview, key indication, hero data point
2. Efficacy & clinical trials — trial results with statistics, data callouts
3. Safety profile — adverse events, contraindications, special populations
4. Dosing & administration — dosing table, preparation, device instructions
5. Mechanism of action — how it works, target pathway
6. Resources — HCP materials, patient support tools, prescribing guides

### 6. Pages 4-6 are off-brand

- **Page 4** (Patient empowerment, support groups, financial assistance) — this is a patient-facing page. AZ HCP sites frame support as "Resources for you and your patients", not "Empowering Patients and the Community"
- **Page 5** (Future R&D pipeline, digital solutions, global access) — this is investor/corporate content. AZ product sites focus on the launched product, not speculative future research
- **Page 6** (Social media, newsletter signup, customer service reps) — this is a consumer engagement model. AZ HCP sites use "Contact us" and "Medical Information" links, not social media mosaics

## Voice & Copy Issues

### 7. Heading capitalisation is wrong

AZ uses sentence case for all headings. The briefing uses Title Case throughout:
- "The Eluvion Blueprint" → should be "The Eluvion blueprint" (and this heading itself is too literary)
- "Decoding the Science of a Breakthrough" → should be something like "Understanding the mechanism of action"
- "A Story of Rigour and Patient-Centric Research" → "Efficacy and clinical trials"

### 8. Vocabulary violates AZ conventions

| Briefing uses | AZ prefers |
|---|---|
| "groundbreaking new therapy" | "indicated for the treatment of" |
| "poised to make a significant impact" | "significantly reduces [outcome]" |
| "unveiling a novel therapy" | "discover the benefits of" |
| "a testament to our commitment" | (omit — self-congratulatory) |
| "countless individuals" | "adult patients with [condition]" |
| "offrant" (typo) | "offering" |
| "Targetted" (typo) | "targeted" |

### 9. CTAs are not action-oriented

AZ CTAs use imperative verbs with specific objects: "Explore efficacy data", "View safety profile", "See dosing guide". The briefing uses aspirational slogans:
- "Explore the Science. Empowering Patients. Transforming Futures." — not a CTA
- "Interactive Molecular Model, Detailed Explanations" — feature list, not CTAs
- "Stay in the Know" — colloquial, violates vocabulary rules

## Imagery Issues

### 10. Does not follow AZ 4-tier imagery system

The briefing's image descriptions don't map to any AZ imagery tier:

| Briefing image | Issue | AZ tier it should use |
|---|---|---|
| "Futuristic architectural concept image of an R&D facility" | Sci-fi aesthetic, not AZ | Tier 1: Double-exposure artistic portrait |
| "Scientist focused on microscope" | Lab/clinical setting — explicit anti-reference | Tier 2: Warm lifestyle photography |
| "Clinical trial participant holding a pamphlet" | Patient testimonial imagery, consumer-facing | Tier 2 or omit entirely |
| "Customer service representative with headset" | Consumer support aesthetic | Not appropriate for HCP site |
| "Hand clicking Subscribe button" | Consumer engagement imagery | Not appropriate for HCP site |
| "World map of clinical trials" | Infographic — acceptable but not AZ style | Data visualisation pattern |
| "Radiologist analyzing an image" | Clinical/hospital setting | Tier 2: Warm lifestyle outdoors |

**Correct AZ imagery approach:**
- Hero: Double-exposure portrait silhouette filled with scientific imagery in product brand colour (Tier 1)
- Product sections: Warm lifestyle photography of diverse patients in everyday settings (Tier 2)
- Device: Clean product shots on white background (Tier 3)
- No labs, no hospitals, no microscopes, no headsets

### 11. No product brand colour defined

Every AZ product has an accent colour that influences imagery, icons, and highlights. The briefing doesn't define one for Eluvion. This should be specified (e.g., if Eluvion is an oncology product, a dramatic teal/navy or purple; if respiratory, a blue/teal).
