Safety profile of TRELUXIA

TRELUXIA™ (trelimovab) has been evaluated in over 700 patients across the LUMINOS clinical programme, with treatment durations of up to 52 weeks.¹ ² ³

A favourable safety profile consistent with a targeted biologic

The overall safety profile of TRELUXIA is consistent with expectations for a human IgG2 monoclonal antibody targeting an epithelial cytokine. Serious adverse events were lower with TRELUXIA than placebo (8.4% vs 11.2%), driven by fewer asthma-related hospitalisations. Treatment discontinuation due to adverse events was low (2.4% vs 3.1%). The most common adverse events were mild upper respiratory tract infections and injection site reactions.¹ ² ³

Safety profile across the LUMINOS programme

Across pooled LUMINOS data (N=732 TRELUXIA), the adverse event profile was dominated by common respiratory infections and mild injection site reactions. No clinically significant immunosuppression, malignancy signal, or cardiovascular safety concerns were identified. Anti-drug antibodies were detected in 5.1% of patients but had no impact on efficacy or safety. Anaphylaxis was rare (0.4%) and all events resolved with standard management.¹ ² ³

Injection site reactions

Injection site reactions occurred in 4.6% of TRELUXIA-treated patients vs 2.8% with placebo. The most common were erythema, pain, and swelling. All ISRs were mild to moderate in severity. No injection site reaction led to treatment discontinuation.³

Anaphylaxis and hypersensitivity

Anaphylaxis was reported in 3 patients (0.4%) receiving TRELUXIA. All events occurred within 24 hours of injection and resolved with standard treatment. The first 3 doses should be administered under healthcare professional supervision. Patients should carry an adrenaline auto-injector for the duration of treatment.³

Infections and immunogenicity

Upper respiratory tract infections were the most common adverse events, occurring at similar rates in TRELUXIA and placebo groups. No clinically significant immunosuppression was observed. Anti-drug antibodies were detected in 5.1% but had no impact on efficacy, safety, or pharmacokinetics.¹ ³

Special populations

Adolescents (12–17 years): Same dose as adults (210 mg Q4W). In LUMINOS-1, 63 adolescents received TRELUXIA with consistent efficacy and safety.³

Elderly patients: No dose adjustment required in patients aged ≥65 years. 11% of LUMINOS-1 patients were ≥65 years.³

Pregnancy and fertility: TRELUXIA should only be used during pregnancy if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the foetus. Human IgG antibodies cross the placental barrier.³

Renal/hepatic impairment: No dose adjustment for mild to moderate impairment. Not studied in severe impairment.³

Prescribing Information

TRELUXIA (trelimovab) 210 mg/1.91 mL solution for injection in pre-filled syringe and pre-filled pen. Please refer to the Summary of Product Characteristics (SmPC) before prescribing. Indication: Add-on maintenance treatment for adults and adolescents aged ≥12 years with severe asthma that is inadequately controlled despite high-dose inhaled corticosteroids plus at least one additional controller, irrespective of inflammatory phenotype. Dosage and administration: 210 mg administered by subcutaneous injection once every 4 weeks. Administer into the thigh, abdomen (excluding 5 cm around the navel), or upper arm (if administered by a caregiver). Rotate injection sites. Do not inject into areas that are tender, bruised, erythematous, or indurated. The first 3 doses should be administered under the supervision of a healthcare professional. Thereafter, a patient or caregiver may administer TRELUXIA after training in subcutaneous injection technique. Contraindications: Hypersensitivity to trelimovab or any excipient. Warnings and precautions: Do not use to treat acute asthma symptoms or acute exacerbations. Do not abruptly discontinue systemic or inhaled corticosteroids upon initiation of TRELUXIA. Reduce corticosteroids gradually under medical supervision. Hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylaxis have been reported. If a serious hypersensitivity reaction occurs, discontinue TRELUXIA and initiate appropriate therapy. Treat pre-existing helminth infections before initiating TRELUXIA. Interactions: No formal drug interaction studies have been conducted. Population pharmacokinetic analyses indicated that commonly used asthma medications (ICS, LABA, LTRA, LAMA, OCS, theophylline) did not affect the pharmacokinetics of trelimovab. Side effects: Very common (≥1/10): nasopharyngitis. Common (≥1/100 to <1/10): upper respiratory tract infection, headache, injection site reactions, arthralgia. Uncommon (≥1/1000 to <1/100): anaphylaxis, pharyngitis. Legal category: POM. Pack and price: 1 pre-filled syringe (210 mg): £1,267.00; 1 pre-filled pen (210 mg): £1,267.00. Marketing authorisation holder: AstraZeneca UK Ltd. MA number: PLGB 17901/0643. Full prescribing information available from: AstraZeneca UK Ltd, 2 Pancras Square, London N1C 4AG.
